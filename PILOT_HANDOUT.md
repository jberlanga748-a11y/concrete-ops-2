# Concrete Ops 10-Day Founding Pilot

## 1. Goal Of The Pilot

The goal is to prove one real job workflow from the office to the field and back.

We are not trying to set up every possible feature on day one. We want to see whether Concrete Ops helps your team manage one real job with less back-and-forth, better field documentation, and clearer job records.

## 2. What Concrete Ops Helps Manage

Concrete Ops helps concrete contractors organize:

- Leads
- Estimates
- Jobs
- Crews
- Daily reports
- Jobsite photos
- Delivery tickets
- Safety notes and incidents
- Pre-pour checklists
- Post-pour checklists
- Job packets
- Follow-ups

## 3. What John Will Set Up

Before or during the setup call, John will help set up:

- Company info
- Owner/admin user
- One foreman
- One employee
- One customer
- One lead
- One estimate
- One active job
- Crew assignment
- One example jobsite photo
- One example daily report
- One example delivery ticket
- One example checklist

## 4. Day 1 Actions

Admin:

- Review the lead.
- Review the customer.
- Review the estimate.
- Review the active job.
- Confirm the crew assignment is right.

Foreman:

- Log in on a phone.
- Open the assigned job.
- Upload one jobsite photo.
- Submit one daily report.
- Add one delivery ticket if it applies.
- Complete the relevant pre-pour or post-pour checklist.

Employee:

- Log in on a phone.
- View the assigned job.
- Use only the field tools available to the employee role.
- Tell John if anything looks confusing or out of place.

## 5. What Field Users Should Not See

Foremen and employees should not see:

- Pricing
- Payroll
- Profit or margin
- Settings
- Leads
- Full customer records
- Estimates
- Office-only notes

If a field user sees something they should not, tell John immediately.

## 6. What To Tell John Immediately

Please tell John right away if you run into:

- Login problems
- A missing job
- The wrong job showing up
- Wrong permissions
- Photo upload confusion
- Daily report confusion
- Checklist confusion
- Delivery ticket confusion
- Anything that feels too slow
- Anything that makes the crew stop using it

## 7. Day 3 Feedback Questions

After a few days, John will ask:

- What did you actually use?
- What did you ignore?
- What was confusing?
- Did field users know what to tap next?
- Did daily reports help?
- Did photos help?
- Did delivery tickets help?
- Did checklists help or get in the way?
- Did anyone see information they should not have seen?

## 8. End-Of-Pilot Decision

At the end of the 10-day pilot, we want honest answers:

- What must improve before paying for this?
- Would you use Concrete Ops on the next real job?
- Would you recommend it to another concrete contractor?

The pilot is successful if Concrete Ops makes real job communication, field documentation, and closeout records easier for your team.
