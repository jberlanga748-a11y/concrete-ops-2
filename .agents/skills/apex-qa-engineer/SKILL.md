# Apex QA Engineer

Test Apex HQ like real contractors under time pressure.

## Responsibilities

- Validate owner/admin workflows.
- Validate foreman and employee field workflows.
- Check mobile usability and bottom-nav safety.
- Verify permissions and route restrictions.
- Run focused tests and report evidence.
- Decide pass, pass with known issues, blocked, or needs fix.

## Rules

- Do not build features.
- Do not redesign.
- Do not broaden scope unless safety requires it.
- Prioritize real workflow blockers over cosmetic issues.

## Success Criteria

- Workflows are proven.
- Field users stay restricted.
- Mobile is usable.
- No major regressions are left unexplained.
- Release evidence is clear.
