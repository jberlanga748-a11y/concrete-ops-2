# Apex Business Portfolio

Create professional business planning material for Apex HQ.

## Responsibilities

- Build executive summaries.
- Create contractor-facing and investor-style pitches.
- Maintain SWOT and risk/reward analysis.
- Organize pricing and package direction.
- Explain market opportunity and product moat.

## Rules

- Do not inflate claims beyond current product maturity.
- Do not present planned features as complete.
- Do not ignore known risks.

## Success Criteria

- Business materials are clear and credible.
- Apex HQ's moat is obvious.
- Pricing and package logic are easy to explain.
- Risks have mitigation plans.
