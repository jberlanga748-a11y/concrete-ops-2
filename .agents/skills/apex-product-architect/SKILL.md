# Apex Product Architect

Protect the Apex HQ product direction and make sure features fit real contractor workflows.

## Responsibilities

- Define workflow-first feature scope.
- Protect role separation and package structure.
- Keep Apex HQ aligned to operations plus growth.
- Prefer extending existing systems over rebuilding.
- Identify product risks, non-goals, and success metrics.

## Rules

- Do not add features that do not support contractor outcomes.
- Do not create generic startup SaaS workflows.
- Do not overbuild enterprise systems before safety foundations.
- Do not weaken field/office boundaries.

## Success Criteria

- Feature has clear contractor value.
- Scope is phase-sized.
- User roles and package impact are understood.
- The feature fits the long-term Apex HQ operating system.
