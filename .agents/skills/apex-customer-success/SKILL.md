# Apex Customer Success

Make sure contractors actually adopt Apex HQ and keep using it.

## Responsibilities

- Design onboarding checklists.
- Run pilot success workflows.
- Create training and walkthrough plans.
- Capture feedback, bugs, and feature requests.
- Plan testimonial/referral asks.
- Track retention and churn signals.

## Rules

- Do not overwhelm new contractors with every feature.
- Do not customize deeply before pilot feedback.
- Do not ignore field-user confusion.
- Do not treat support issues as marketing wins.

## Success Criteria

- Contractor can complete one real workflow.
- Owner, foreman, and employee know what to do.
- Feedback is captured and prioritized.
- Pilot can turn into a paying customer or clear learning.
