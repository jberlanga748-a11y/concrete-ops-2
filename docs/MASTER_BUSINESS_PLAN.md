# Apex HQ Master Business Plan

Status: working business plan
Product stage: pilot-grade, not public-SaaS-ready

## Executive Summary

Apex HQ is a contractor operations and growth platform. It helps contractors organize leads, estimates, jobs, crews, field proof, reports, follow-ups, and payment-readiness work in one contractor command center.

The first business goal is not to sell to everyone. The first goal is to prove one repeatable workflow with real contractors:

```text
Estimate or job setup -> crew/field handoff -> photos/report/proof -> owner review -> follow-up
```

## Business Mission

Help contractors run cleaner companies, win more professional work, reduce field/office chaos, and grow without losing control.

## Founder Advantage

Apex HQ has a founder-market fit advantage.

John Berlanga has 15 years of concrete experience, from field work to running the business side. That matters because Apex HQ is not being built from a generic software checklist. It is being built around real contractor friction:

- job details living in texts
- photos scattered across phones
- crews needing simple field workflows
- owners chasing updates
- estimates turning into unclear handoffs
- proof and follow-up affecting payment

This experience should be used in sales and marketing as credibility, but not as ego. The message is:

```text
I have lived the problem, so I am building Apex HQ around how contractors actually work.
```

## Customer Problem

Small and mid-size contractors often run the business across:

- texts
- spreadsheets
- paper notes
- phone photos
- memory
- disconnected software
- owner-only knowledge

This creates:

- missed follow-ups
- weak estimate handoff
- lost job photos
- unclear crew updates
- poor daily proof
- change order disputes
- slower billing
- owner burnout

## Target Market

First niche:

- concrete contractors
- hardscape contractors
- excavation contractors
- small GCs with field crews
- remodelers with project-based work

Best early customer:

- owner-led
- 2 to 25 field workers
- uses phones in the field
- has active estimates/jobs
- feels pain around job handoff, photos, reports, and follow-up

## Value Proposition

Apex HQ gives contractors one place to see what work is coming in, what needs priced, what crews are doing, what proof is missing, and what needs follow-up.

Plain version:

```text
Stop running jobs out of texts and memory.
```

## Product Positioning

Apex HQ is not just:

- CRM
- proposal app
- photo app
- scheduling app
- field app

Apex HQ is becoming:

- contractor operations system
- contractor growth system
- contractor marketing support system
- contractor business intelligence layer
- contractor AI assistant platform

But early sales should stay focused on the practical pilot workflow.

## Revenue Model

Primary:

- monthly subscriptions
- annual subscriptions
- setup/onboarding fees

Secondary:

- premium onboarding
- proposal/template setup
- website lead intake setup
- growth partner services
- training and rollout support

Do not rely on:

- paid ads before manual conversion is proven
- enterprise deals before trust/security foundations are mature
- AI usage revenue before cost controls are understood

## Package Direction

| Package | Best Fit | Business Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic | small contractor that needs organization | entry plan |
| Premium | growing contractor with real office/field pain | main plan to sell |
| Elite | contractor wanting hands-on setup/growth help | service-heavy premium plan |

Recommended default:

Sell Premium after the founder pilot if Apex HQ proves value.

## Go-To-Market Strategy

Phase 1: Founder-led validation.

- manual outreach
- personal calls
- 20-minute demos
- 14-day pilots
- direct feedback
- testimonials only after real value

Phase 2: Repeatable local channel.

- referrals
- local contractor groups
- supplier/material yard relationships
- GC/subcontractor networks
- trade associations
- LinkedIn/Facebook content

Phase 3: Scaled growth.

- case studies
- trade-specific landing pages
- short demo videos
- partner referrals
- paid ads only after manual conversion is proven

## Sales Motion

1. Find contractor with likely office/field pain.
2. Send short outreach.
3. Book 20-minute walkthrough.
4. Diagnose one painful workflow.
5. Offer 14-day pilot.
6. Set up one workflow.
7. Check in on day 3.
8. Review value on day 10.
9. Close to Basic/Premium/Elite or stop cleanly.

## Customer Success Motion

Apex HQ should win by making early customers feel supported.

Minimum success:

- first customer/lead entered
- first estimate/job entered
- first field user understands their action
- first photo/report/proof captured
- owner sees value

## Strategic Risks

- overclaiming readiness
- founder overload
- weak field adoption
- support load
- AI expectation creep
- contractor resistance to software
- selling custom work instead of product
- moving to automation before manual learning

## Strategic Discipline

Do now:

- founder-led outreach
- manual demos
- one-workflow pilots
- business email setup
- customer notes and follow-ups
- referral asks

Do later:

- automated email/text
- paid ads
- full customer portal
- deep integrations
- enterprise promises
- AI calling/answering
- large-company rollouts
