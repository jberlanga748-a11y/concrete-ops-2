## Rules

- Do not log in, post, publish, DM, follow, comment, or contact anyone without John's approval.
- Apex HQ is for contractors and trade businesses, not only concrete or fencing.
- Keep the tone plain, direct, and contractor-first.
- Do not claim customers, revenue, testimonials, case studies, partnerships, press, or legal status that do not exist.
- Do not promise guaranteed leads, guaranteed revenue, AI autopilot, payroll replacement, accounting replacement, or automatic bidding/sending/billing.
- Describe AI as review-first unless John has approved a specific current capability and the risk is clear.
- Use founder-led demos, early access, or controlled rollout language until public self-serve readiness is approved.

## Approval Workflow

1. Agent drafts caption, visual idea, and CTA.
2. John reviews for truth, tone, and current product readiness.
3. John removes anything that sounds too big, too automated, or too polished for the current stage.
4. John manually posts.
5. John logs:
   - post date
   - topic
   - views/reach
   - likes/comments/saves
   - profile visits
   - link clicks
   - DMs or replies
   - conversations started
6. Agent reviews results weekly and adjusts the next batch.

## Response Rules For Comments And DMs

If someone asks "What is Apex HQ?":

```text
Apex HQ is a contractor command center that helps keep leads, estimates, jobs, crews, photos, reports, change orders, and follow-ups connected in one place.
```

If someone asks "Does it get leads?":

```text
It helps organize opportunities and follow-up. I do not promise guaranteed leads. The goal is to help contractors stay on top of the work and opportunities they already have.
```

If someone asks "Does AI run it?":

```text
The AI is review-first. It helps draft, organize, flag, and prepare work, but important actions should still be reviewed before sending, pricing, scheduling, billing, or changing anything.
```

If someone asks "Can I demo it?":

```text
Yes. I am doing founder-led walkthroughs. Send me your trade, company size, and the biggest thing you are trying to organize right now.
```
