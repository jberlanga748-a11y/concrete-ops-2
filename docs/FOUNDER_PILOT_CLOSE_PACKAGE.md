# Apex HQ Founder Pilot Close Package

Status: ready-to-use draft
Use for: turning a good demo into a narrow paid pilot

## Close Position

Do not sell the whole platform on the first close.

Sell one workflow:

```text
lead/estimate -> job setup -> field handoff -> photo/report proof -> owner review -> follow-up
```

The pilot is a test, not a company-wide switch.

## When To Offer A Pilot

Offer a pilot only if:

- the contractor has a real workflow pain
- they understood at least one part of Apex HQ
- they are willing to test one workflow
- support burden looks manageable
- price reaction is not hostile
- the demo did not expose a major blocker

Do not offer a pilot if:

- they only want guaranteed leads
- they need accounting/payroll first
- they want custom features before using anything
- they will not use even one workflow
- they expect public SaaS polish and self-serve support

## Pilot Offer

```text
The cleanest way to test Apex HQ is one real workflow for 14 days. We set it up together, use it on real work, check in after day 3, and then decide if it is worth keeping.
```

Shorter version:

```text
We are not trying to switch your whole company. We pick one workflow, test it on real work, and decide if it actually helps.
```

## Pilot Packages

### Basic Founder Pilot

Best for:

- solo contractor
- owner-operator
- no employees or very light help
- mainly needs leads, estimates, job info, website lead flow, and follow-up

Founder pilot price:

- $99/month
- no long-term contract during founder pilot
- 14-day guided test first if needed

Includes:

- leads
- customers
- estimates/proposals
- basic job info
- follow-ups
- rough notes/AI help where ready
- founder setup help for one workflow

Does not include:

- full crew rollout
- custom website rebuild
- accounting/payroll
- guaranteed leads
- automatic AI sending or approving

### Premium Founder Pilot

Best for:

- growing contractor
- 1 to 5 crews
- owner/admin managing several jobs
- field handoff and proof are becoming messy

Founder pilot price:

- $249/month
- no long-term contract during founder pilot

Includes:

- everything in Basic
- jobs and schedule workflow
- field handoff
- photo/report proof
- delivery tickets
- pre-pour/post-pour where relevant
- owner/admin review workflow
- one foreman or field-user setup

Does not include:

- full company migration
- advanced custom workflows
- unlimited hands-on support
- accounting/payroll replacement

### Elite Founder Support

Best for:

- serious growth-minded contractor
- needs hands-on setup
- wants templates, training, and monthly workflow/growth review
- clear value is at stake

Founder price direction:

- $750+/month
- only offer after need and support capacity are clear

Includes:

- guided setup
- owner/admin workflow design
- basic templates
- training
- monthly review
- growth workflow help

Do not offer Elite first just because someone has money. Offer it only when the support need and value are obvious.

## Close Script

```text
Based on what you told me, I would not start with everything. I would test [workflow] first.

We can do it as a founder pilot: 14 days, one workflow, setup help from me, day-3 check-in, and then we decide if it is worth keeping.

For where you are at, I would put you in [Basic/Premium]. That would be [price]/month if you keep it after the pilot.
```

No-pressure version:

```text
If it saves time, cleans up handoffs, or makes job details easier to review, we keep going. If it does not, we adjust or stop. No long contract and no pretending it solves things it does not.
```

## If They Ask "Can It Get Me Jobs?"

```text
That is the goal, but I do not want to sell it like a lead company promising jobs. Apex HQ is meant to help you find opportunities, follow up faster, make cleaner estimates/proposals, and keep job info organized so you can win more of the work you already come across.
```

## If They Say "I Already Use Wix"

```text
That makes sense. I am not trying to replace your website first. I am looking at what happens after a lead comes in: estimate, follow-up, job info, photos, reports, and keeping everything from getting scattered.
```

## If They Are Solo

```text
Then I would keep it simple. You probably do not need the full crew side right now. I would focus on leads, estimates/proposals, job info, website lead flow, rough notes, and follow-up.
```

## If They Have Crews

```text
Then the bigger value is probably the handoff: estimate to job, schedule, what the field needs to see, photos/reports coming back, and the owner knowing what needs attention.
```

## Success Criteria

Pick 1 to 3 before starting:

- one real estimate is entered or drafted cleaner
- one real job is set up
- one field update/photo/report comes back through the workflow
- owner finds job info faster
- one follow-up is not missed
- contractor says they would pay to keep using it

## Week 1 Setup

Day 0:

- confirm plan
- pick one workflow
- set owner/admin expectations
- choose first real job or estimate

Day 1:

- owner/admin logs in
- workflow is visible
- first estimate/job is reviewed

Day 2:

- one field or proof action happens if relevant
- photos/report/update tested

Day 3:

- check what worked
- check what still went through text
- remove confusion

Day 4-7:

- use on real work
- collect feedback
- do not add new scope unless needed

Day 10:

- value review
- decide continue, adjust, or stop

## Day-3 Check-In Script

```text
Quick check-in. Did you get into Apex HQ? Did the workflow make sense? What still ended up happening through text or paper?
```

Ask:

- what worked
- what confused you
- did it save any chasing
- what needs to be simpler
- should we keep testing this workflow

## Day-10 Value Review Script

```text
Now that you have seen it on real work, did it clean anything up enough to keep using?
```

Ask:

- what did Apex HQ help organize
- what was still clunky
- did it help with estimates, job info, field proof, or follow-up
- would you pay to keep it
- Basic, Premium, or not yet
- who else should see it

## Payment Close

```text
If you want to keep it after the pilot, I would keep you on [plan] at [price]/month as a founder customer. No long-term contract right now. We keep the scope clear and make sure the workflow is actually helping.
```

## What Not To Promise

Do not promise:

- guaranteed jobs
- AI bidding or sending without review
- accounting/payroll replacement
- custom features on demand
- full public SaaS support
- full company migration in the first week
- unlimited one-on-one support for Basic

Do not say:

- "Apex HQ will get you more jobs."
- "This runs your business on autopilot."
- "AI will handle everything for you."
- "You can replace all your tools right away."
- "We can build whatever you need."
- "This is enterprise-ready."
- "Your crews will definitely use it."
- "You will save X hours every week."
- "You will close more bids automatically."
