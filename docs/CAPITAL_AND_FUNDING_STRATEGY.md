# Apex HQ Capital And Funding Strategy

Status: business planning, not financial advice

## Default Strategy

Bootstrap as long as possible.

Why:

- founder has domain experience
- early sales can be founder-led
- setup/service revenue can fund growth
- customer proof is more valuable than pitch decks right now

## Cash Sources

Best early:

- Premium subscriptions
- setup fees
- Elite service packages
- annual prepay after trust exists

Possible later:

- small business credit line
- strategic partner investment
- angel investment
- accelerator
- revenue-based financing

Avoid early:

- expensive debt before revenue
- giving up equity before customer proof
- ad spend funded by credit
- hiring ahead of repeatable sales

## Funding Milestones

Before outside funding:

- 10+ paying customers
- Premium accepted
- repeatable demo
- 2+ testimonials/case studies
- clear churn risks
- clear use of funds

Better milestone:

- $10K+ MRR
- predictable acquisition channel
- onboarding process working
- support load understood

## Use Of Funds If Raised Later

Good uses:

- sales/admin support
- customer success
- product hardening
- security/trust work
- approved marketing tests

Bad uses:

- vanity branding
- broad ads before conversion proof
- custom features for one customer
- premature office overhead

## Cash Discipline

Track monthly:

- MRR
- setup revenue
- churn
- support hours
- software/tool costs
- AI/API costs
- hosting/storage
- sales activity

Rule:

```text
Do not spend like a SaaS company until customers prove Apex HQ is one.
```
