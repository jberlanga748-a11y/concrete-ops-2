## Headline

Built From 15 Years In Concrete

## Short Version

John Berlanga started Apex HQ after 15 years in concrete, from field work to the business side.

He has seen what happens when estimates, job notes, photos, reports, crew updates, and follow-ups are spread across texts, paper, spreadsheets, and memory.

Apex HQ is being built to clean up that workflow for contractors.

## Contractor-Facing Story

I have spent 15 years in concrete. I have been the field guy, and I have been on the business side trying to keep the work moving.

I know what it feels like when job details are buried in texts, photos are stuck on different phones, reports are missing, and the owner has to chase every update.

That is why Apex HQ is being built around the real contractor workflow:

```text
lead/estimate -> job setup -> field handoff -> photo/report proof -> owner review -> follow-up
```

The goal is not to make software that looks impressive in a boardroom. The goal is to help contractors keep the work organized enough to run cleaner jobs, follow up better, and grow without losing control.

## What I Am Looking For

I am opening founder-led demos with a small group of contractors first.

Good fit if:

- you estimate contractor work
- you manage jobs or crews
- photos, notes, and follow-ups get scattered
- you want a practical workflow, not bloated software
- you are willing to give honest feedback

## What I Am Not Claiming

Apex HQ is not claiming:

- guaranteed leads
- guaranteed jobs
- fully automated AI
- accounting/payroll replacement
- public self-serve SaaS readiness
- enterprise rollout
