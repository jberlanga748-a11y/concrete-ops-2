## The Problem

Most contractors are not short on effort. The problem is that the work gets scattered.

Leads sit in one place. Estimates are in another. Job notes are in texts. Photos are stuck on phones. Reports are missing. Follow-ups live in somebody's head. Then the owner has to chase the crew, the office, the customer, and the paperwork just to know what happened.

That slows down estimates, handoffs, proof, billing readiness, and customer communication.

## What Apex HQ Does

Apex HQ helps contractors keep the office and field on the same page.

It is being built around the real contractor workflow:

```text
Win the work -> price the work -> run the work -> prove the work -> review the work -> follow up
```

It can help organize:

- leads and customers
- estimates and follow-ups
- jobs and crew assignments
- field photos and reports
- proof for completed work
- change notes and owner review
- handoffs between office and field

## Who It Is For

## FAQ

### Is Apex HQ ready to replace all of our systems?

No. The founder pilot is meant to test one useful workflow first. It is not a full company migration.

### Do we have to move our whole crew onto it?

No. A pilot can start with one owner/admin and one field user or foreman.

### Does Apex HQ guarantee more leads?

No. Apex HQ is focused on organizing contractor work and follow-up. It does not guarantee lead volume or sales.

### Does it send messages automatically?

Not in the founder pilot. Communication should stay review-first so nothing is sent without a human checking it.

### Does it replace accounting, payroll, or project management software?

No. Apex HQ is not being sold as an accounting or payroll replacement. It is focused on estimates, jobs, crews, field proof, review, and follow-up.

### What makes Apex HQ different?

It is being built by a concrete founder around real contractor operations, not generic office software. The pilot stays practical: one workflow, real feedback, and an honest decision after testing.
