# Apex HQ Founder Story

Status: founder credibility copy
Founder: John Berlanga

## Short Founder Story

John Berlanga has 15 years of concrete experience, from being the field guy to running the business side.

Apex HQ is being built from that experience. It is for contractors who are tired of estimates, job details, photos, reports, crew updates, and follow-ups getting scattered across texts, paper, spreadsheets, and memory.

## Contractor-Facing Version

```text
I have spent 15 years in concrete, from being the field guy to running the business side. I know what it is like when job details live in texts, photos are stuck on phones, reports are missing, and the owner has to chase everything.

Apex HQ is being built around that real contractor workflow: estimate, job, crew, field proof, review, and follow-up.
```

## Short Outreach Version

```text
I have 15 years in concrete, from the field to the business side, and I am building Apex HQ around the job tracking and field proof problems contractors deal with every day.
```

## Demo Version

```text
I am not coming at this as a generic software pitch. I have 15 years in concrete, from field work to running the business side. The reason Apex HQ focuses on estimates, jobs, crews, photos, reports, and follow-ups is because that is where things get messy fast.
```

## Website About Version

Apex HQ was started by John Berlanga after 15 years in concrete, from hands-on field work to running the business side.

The product is being built around real contractor operations: winning work, pricing work, handing it to the field, documenting what happened, following up, and getting paid with better proof.

## What This Should Communicate

- Apex HQ understands field reality.
- The founder knows concrete workflows firsthand.
- The product is not generic startup software.
- The company respects contractors' time.
- The field side must stay simple.
- The owner/admin side must reduce chasing.

## What Not To Do

- do not make the founder story sound like bragging
- do not claim this makes Apex HQ better than mature competitors by itself
- do not overstate app readiness
- do not pretend every trade is already deeply supported
- do not use the story to dodge hard product questions

## Best Line

```text
Built from 15 years of concrete field and business experience.
```
