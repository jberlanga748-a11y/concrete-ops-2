# Apex HQ Demo Launch Packet

Status: guided-demo packet; current app readiness must be confirmed through `docs/APEX_HQ_BUILD_STATUS_AND_PHASES.md` and `docs/MANUAL_PILOT_SMOKE_TEST.md`
Use for: the first founder-led demos and controlled pilot conversations

## Launch Position

Apex HQ is not being launched as public self-serve SaaS yet.

Use this position:

```text
Apex HQ is a contractor operations platform entering a controlled founder-led demo and pilot phase.
```

Plain version:

```text
I built Apex HQ to help contractors keep leads, estimates, jobs, crews, photos, reports, tickets, safety items, and follow-ups organized in one place.
```

## Who To Invite First

Start with warm and local contractors before cold outreach.

Priority order:

1. Contractors John already knows.
2. Contractors who already replied with interest, like M2 Mini.
3. Local owner-operators using Wix, Facebook, paper, texts, or handwritten estimates.
4. Concrete, hardscape, excavation, fencing, siding, framing, remodel, and small GC companies.
5. Contractors with 1 to 5 crews who are growing but still owner-managed.

Avoid first:

- contractors asking for guaranteed leads
- contractors needing accounting/payroll right away
- companies wanting custom software before trying the core workflow
- enterprise companies with long procurement
- anyone who will not give honest feedback

## Send Order

Use this order for the first clean-demo week:

1. M2 Mini follow-up.
2. 4 warm contractor texts.
3. 5 researched local contractor emails.
4. 5 calls to the same warm/local group.
5. Follow up with everyone who replies.
6. Book only 15-minute guided demos at first.
7. Offer pilot only after they point to a real workflow pain.

## What Not To Say

Do not say:

- guaranteed jobs
- AI gets you work automatically
- fully launched public SaaS
- replaces QuickBooks
- replaces payroll
- sends texts/emails for you automatically
- enterprise-ready

Say:

```text
The goal is to help contractors find opportunities, follow up faster, organize job details, and win more of the work they already come across.
```
