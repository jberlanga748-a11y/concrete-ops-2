# Apex HQ Outreach Text and Call Drafts

Status: Prep-only outreach copy
Sending: Paused
Audience: First 20 outreach targets from `docs/OUTREACH_SPRINT_WORKSPACE.md`
Primary CTA: 20-minute Apex HQ walkthrough

## Guardrails

- Do not send from this file.
- Do not use personal Gmail.
- Do not claim Apex HQ is a lead service.
- Do not promise leads, revenue, savings, automation, or finished features.
- Keep the ask small: a 20-minute walkthrough and honest contractor feedback.
- Use plain contractor language: jobs, estimates, crews, photos, reports, follow-ups, office-to-field handoff.
- If asked what is live, be direct: "We are opening a small founder pilot and showing the workflow before bringing on the right early users."
- Position Apex HQ as guided-demo ready, not public self-serve SaaS.
- Mention AI only as review-first help, not as a guarantee.

## Reusable SMS Scripts

### First SMS - General

Hey [Name/team], saw [company] handles [trade] around [area]. I am opening a small Apex HQ founder pilot for contractors who want estimates, jobs, photos, reports, and follow-ups easier to keep track of. Open to a 20-minute walkthrough?

### First SMS - Small Contractor

Hey [Name/team], quick one. Saw [company] handles [trade] around [area]. I am showing a few contractors Apex HQ, built around keeping estimates, job notes, photos, and follow-ups from getting buried in texts. Want to take a 20-minute look?

### First SMS - Larger Contractor

Hey [Name/team], saw [company] handles [trade/work type]. I am opening a few Apex HQ pilot conversations around job visibility, field proof, crew notes, and follow-up tracking. Would someone on your estimating or ops side be open to a 20-minute walkthrough?

### Reply When Interested

Sounds good. I will keep it practical: estimate to job, crew handoff, photos/report, and follow-up. I have [day/time] or [day/time] open. Which is better?

### Reply When They Ask "What Is Apex HQ?"

Apex HQ is a contractor operations workflow we are piloting. The goal is simple: less chasing between estimates, job details, field photos, reports, and follow-ups. Not a lead service and not asking you to switch everything.

### Reply When They Ask "Is It Ready?"

The app is live and we are doing guided founder-led demos now. I am not opening it as public self-serve yet. I am showing it to a small group of contractors first and only setting up pilots where one workflow clearly fits.

### Reply When They Are Busy

Totally fair. Want me to check back [next week/in two weeks], or should I send two walkthrough times and you can pick one if it fits?

### Reply When They Say They Already Have Software

Makes sense. I am not trying to replace everything. The walkthrough is mainly to see where estimate handoff, job photos, reports, or follow-ups still get messy and whether one focused pilot workflow would help.

### Reply When They Ask Cost

It depends on company size. A solo contractor would likely start around $99/month during the founder pilot. A growing contractor with crews would likely be closer to $249/month. I would rather match the package to what you would actually use instead of overselling you.

### Reply When They Ask About AI

The AI side is meant to help, not take over. It can help sort opportunities, turn rough notes into cleaner drafts, and organize the office side. You still review anything important before it goes out or becomes real.

Quick follow-up on Apex HQ. [Company] looked like a good fit because [specific reason]. Worth a 20-minute look, or should I close the loop?

### Follow-Up 2

The main thing I wanted to show is how Apex HQ is being built to keep estimate details, job notes, field photos, reports, and follow-ups together. No pressure to switch everything. Want me to send a couple walkthrough times?

### Follow-Up 3

Last note from me. I am keeping the founder pilot small and contractor-focused. If now is not a fit, no worries. If job tracking or field proof becomes a headache later, I would be happy to show you Apex HQ.

## Reusable Call Scripts

### Cold Call Opener

"Hey, this is John with Apex HQ. I saw [company] does [trade/work type] around [area]. I am reaching out because I am opening a small founder pilot for contractors who want estimates, jobs, crews, photos, reports, and follow-ups easier to keep track of. Did I catch you at a bad time?"

### If They Give You 30 Seconds

"Appreciate it. Apex HQ is not a lead service, and I am not asking you to switch everything. I am looking for a few contractors to walk through one real workflow, like estimate to job handoff or field photos and reports, and tell me if it would actually help."

### If They Ask What It Does

"The short version is contractor job organization. The workflow is being built around estimate details, job notes, crew handoff, photos, reports, and follow-ups, so less gets lost between the office, the field, and the customer."

### If They Are Skeptical

"That is fair. A lot of software pitches are too much. This is meant to be a practical walkthrough, not a big switch. If it is not useful after 20 minutes, we call it there."

### Qualifying Questions

- "How are you tracking estimate follow-ups today?"
- "Where do job photos and daily notes usually end up?"
- "Who has to chase crews or customers the most?"
- "What gets messy between estimate, scheduling, and the actual job?"
- "If you could clean up one workflow this month, what would it be?"

### Soft Close

"Would it be worth a 20-minute walkthrough? I can show the workflow, you can tell me where it is useful or where it misses."

### Booking Close

"I have [day/time] or [day/time]. Which one is better for a quick walkthrough?"

### Gatekeeper Line

"I am trying to reach whoever handles estimating, operations, or owner-level job tracking. It is about a small Apex HQ founder pilot for contractors, not a sales lead service."
