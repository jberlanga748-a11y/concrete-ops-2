# Apex HQ Million-Dollar Growth Plan

Status: business planning
Goal: build toward $1M+ annual revenue without overclaiming readiness

## The Target

$1M ARR means about:

```text
$83,333 MRR
```

Apex HQ can reach that through subscriptions plus setup/services.

## Revenue Paths To $1M ARR

| Mix | Approx Customers | Approx MRR |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| All Basic at $99 | 842 | $83,358 |
| All Premium at $249 | 335 | $83,415 |
| All Premium at $399 | 209 | $83,391 |
| 250 Premium at $249 + 25 Elite at $799 | 275 | $82,225 |
| 200 Premium at $299 + 30 Elite at $999 | 230 | $89,770 |
| 150 Premium at $399 + 35 Elite at $1,299 | 185 | $105,315 |

Best practical path:

```text
200-300 strong customers, mostly Premium, with selective Elite accounts.
```

Do not try to reach $1M ARR with cheap Basic alone. It creates too much support load for too little margin.

## Stage 1: Prove The Sale

Target:

- 10 pilots
- 3 to 5 paid customers
- 2 testimonials
- repeatable demo
- proof that Premium pricing is realistic

Main work:

- founder calls
- manual outreach
- one-workflow pilots
- follow-up discipline
- objection tracking

Pass criteria:

- at least 30% of qualified pilots convert
- at least one customer pays Premium
- at least one customer says Apex HQ reduced chasing

## Stage 2: Reach $10K MRR

Target:

- 25 to 50 customers depending on mix
- clear sales script
- onboarding checklist working
- support issues repeatable

Possible mix:

- 30 Premium at $249 = $7,470 MRR
- 5 Elite at $799 = $3,995 MRR
- total = $11,465 MRR

Main work:

- referrals
- supplier/material yard relationships
- trade group content
- customer success rhythm
- case studies

Pass criteria:

- owner can onboard customers without custom chaos
- churn is low enough to keep compounding
- setup fees cover high-touch onboarding

## Stage 3: Reach $25K MRR

Target:

- 75 to 100 customers depending on mix
- repeatable customer acquisition channel
- first lightweight sales/help process

Possible mix:

- 75 Premium at $249 = $18,675 MRR
- 8 Elite at $999 = $7,992 MRR
- total = $26,667 MRR

Main work:

- tighten onboarding
- create demo videos
- build partner/referral flywheel
- start selective paid testing only if manual conversion is proven
- document support playbooks

Pass criteria:

- founder is not the only person who can explain the product
- customer success process is repeatable
- package boundaries are clear

## Stage 4: Reach $50K MRR

Target:

- 150 to 200 customers depending on mix
- stronger product maturity
- sales/customer success help likely needed

Possible mix:

- 150 Premium at $249 = $37,350 MRR
- 15 Elite at $999 = $14,985 MRR
- total = $52,335 MRR

Main work:

- hire or contract support/sales help
- formalize onboarding
- expand partner channels
- add approved automation carefully
- improve retention and expansion

Pass criteria:

- customer support does not break the founder
- onboarding time per customer goes down
- customers renew because the workflow is sticky

## Stage 5: Reach $83K+ MRR

Target:

- $1M+ ARR
- 200 to 350 paid customers depending on price/mix
- repeatable acquisition, onboarding, and retention

Possible winning mix:

- 225 Premium at $299 = $67,275 MRR
- 20 Elite at $999 = $19,980 MRR
- total = $87,255 MRR

Main work:

- proven acquisition channel
- strong product reliability
- customer success system
- retention reporting
- expansion offers
- partner program

## What Must Be True For $1M+

- Premium must be the main plan.
- Setup fees must cover early handholding.
- Contractors must adopt at least one field workflow.
- Apex HQ must reduce owner/admin chasing.
- Product trust must be strong enough for real business data.
- Customer success must prevent churn.
- The business must avoid custom-work traps.
- Marketing must create repeatable demo bookings.

## What Could Stop It

- selling before readiness
- weak onboarding
- field users do not adopt
- too much support per customer
- pricing too low
- unclear niche
- no repeatable lead source
- product trust issues
- founder burns out

## Million-Dollar Rule

Do not chase every contractor.

Win a narrow group first:

```text
contractors with active jobs, field crews, scattered proof, and an owner tired of chasing updates.
```
