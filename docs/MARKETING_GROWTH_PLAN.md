# Apex HQ Marketing And Growth Plan

## Positioning

Apex HQ is a contractor operations and growth platform.

It helps contractors get work, win work, run work, prove work, reduce risk, and get paid faster without spreading the business across texts, spreadsheets, paper notes, photo folders, and memory.

Founder credibility:

John Berlanga has 15 years of concrete experience, from being the field guy to running the business side. Apex HQ should use that as a practical trust point: this is being built by someone who understands concrete, crews, job handoff, field proof, and owner pressure firsthand.

## Plain-English Category

Contractor command center.

Longer version: Apex HQ is a contractor operating system for owners, office/admin users, estimators, foremen, and crews.

## Who It Is For

Best first-fit customers:

- concrete contractors
- excavation contractors
- hardscape contractors
- small GCs with self-performed trade work
- remodelers with field crews
- owner-led contractors with 2 to 25 field workers
- contractors already using phones in the field
- companies with repeat pain around estimates, job notes, photos, daily reports, change orders, and follow-ups

Poor first-fit customers:

- contractors who refuse to use any field software
- companies that need enterprise controls before pilot value is proven
- companies that only want accounting/payroll
- contractors asking for deep custom features before using the core workflow

## Why Contractors Need It

Most small and mid-size contractors do not have one clean operating system. They have leads in texts, estimates in spreadsheets, customer notes in memory, job photos on phones, daily reports on paper, crew updates in group chats, and change orders handled too late.

Apex HQ sells the outcome:

- fewer missed follow-ups
- cleaner estimate-to-job handoff
- better field proof
- less owner chasing
- more professional customer/GC communication
- faster billing readiness
- less risk when a customer questions work

## What Makes Apex HQ Different

Apex HQ is not just a CRM, proposal tool, field app, photo app, or crew tracker. It connects the business loop:

Win the work -> price the work -> run the work -> prove the work -> approve the work -> get paid.

Early differentiation:

- contractor-first language and workflows
- office and field in one system
- proof-of-work tied to job status and billing readiness
- concrete/trade-specific workflows without becoming too narrow
- owner/admin control with protected field roles
- practical AI direction with human approval, not autopilot promises
- founder-led setup and workflow help for early customers

## Why Now

Contractors are under more pressure to respond fast, look professional, document work, protect payment, and run crews without adding office staff. At the same time, AI and mobile workflows are becoming normal, but most contractor software still feels either too generic, too heavy, or too disconnected from field reality.

Apex HQ should position now as a practical tool for contractors who are tired of work getting lost between the estimate, the job, the field, and the invoice.

## Core Message

Stop running jobs out of texts and memory.

Apex HQ keeps leads, estimates, jobs, crews, photos, reports, and follow-ups in one contractor command center so the office and field stay on the same page.

## Short Pitch

Apex HQ gives contractors one place to manage leads, estimates, jobs, crews, field reports, photos, safety, change orders, and follow-ups without losing control between the office and the field.

Founder-led version:

I have spent 15 years in concrete, from the field to running the business side. Apex HQ is the system I wish contractors had for keeping estimates, jobs, crews, photos, reports, and follow-ups from getting scattered everywhere.

## Contractor-Facing Pitch

You should not have to chase photos, reports, tickets, crew updates, and follow-ups through texts and paper notes. Apex HQ keeps the office and field on the same page so you can run cleaner jobs, look more professional, and get paid with better proof.

## Founding Contractor Offer

Target:

- small to mid-size contractors
- concrete first, then excavation, hardscape, remodel, and small GC
- crews that use phones in the field
- companies already losing time to texts, missing photos, missing reports, and scattered estimates

Offer:

- 14-day founding pilot
- setup help included
- one real workflow: lead or customer -> estimate -> job -> crew -> photos -> daily report -> review
- day-3 feedback call
- day-10 value review
- end-of-pilot decision call
- founder pricing if they continue

Do not promise:

- full accounting replacement
- automatic email/SMS sending
- public customer portal
- enterprise controls
- AI that acts without approval
- guaranteed lead volume

The first 100 should come from proof, referrals, local trust, and practical content before paid ads.

| Channel | Practical Action | Success Metric |

### Scaling Motion

1. Close 10 founder pilots.
2. Turn 3 wins into short case stories.
3. Build one simple demo video for concrete/hardscape.
4. Build one setup offer that is easy to buy.
5. Ask every customer for referrals and a short quote.
6. Run weekly founder outreach blocks until 100 demos are booked.
7. Add paid acquisition only after messaging converts manually.

## Content Strategy

- how to follow up on estimates
- how to run a cleaner concrete job
- how to prepare better GC packets

## Social Strategy

- show practical workflows, not hype
- short before/after examples
- field-to-office pain stories
- demo clips from fake data only
- never show customer data without approval
- use owner language: "less chasing", "cleaner handoffs", "proof in one place"

## Review/Testimonial Strategy

Ask after:

- first job completed
- first successful field report
- first estimate/proposal that looks professional
- first time photos/report helped settle confusion

Testimonial prompt:

What did Apex HQ help you stop chasing or keep better organized?
