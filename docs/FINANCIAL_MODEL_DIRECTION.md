# Apex HQ Financial Model Direction

Status: planning model, not accounting advice

## Business Model

Apex HQ should combine SaaS revenue with setup and premium service revenue.

Why:

- early contractors need help getting started
- setup revenue offsets founder support time
- service revenue can fund product development before scale
- Premium/Elite positioning is stronger when the customer gets workflow help

## Revenue Streams

| Stream | Timing | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic subscription | early | entry plan for small contractors |
| Premium subscription | early/main | primary plan to sell |
| Elite subscription/service | selective | hands-on setup/growth support |
| Setup/onboarding fee | early | helps cover founder time |
| Annual prepay | after proof | improves cash flow |
| Website lead intake setup | later/selected | only when workflow and site offer are ready |
| Growth partner services | later/selected | for Elite customers |

## Recommended Starting Prices

| Package | Monthly | Setup | Founder Pilot |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Basic | $99-$199 | $250-$750 | $99/month after pilot |
| Premium | $249-$499 | $750-$2,500 | $249/month after pilot |
| Elite | $799-$1,500+ | $2,500-$7,500 | custom after workflow proof |

## Cost Watchpoints

| Cost | Risk | Control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Founder support time | high early workload | charge setup, narrow pilots |
| AI/API usage | cost can creep | usage limits, package limits, review-first workflows |
| Storage/uploads | photos/files can grow | monitor storage, package limits |
| Infrastructure | increases with workspaces | track per-customer cost |
| Email/SMS | automation costs and compliance | manual first, approved tools later |
| Paid ads | spend before conversion proof | avoid until manual outreach converts |

## Cash Strategy

First goal:

- use setup revenue to fund onboarding/support time
- use Premium subscriptions to create base MRR
- avoid paid ads until outreach conversion is proven

Good early cash target:

- 3 Premium customers = about $747 MRR
- 5 Premium customers = about $1,245 MRR
- 10 mixed customers = about $1,700-$2,700 MRR

## Break-Even Thinking

Track:

- monthly software costs
- AI/API costs
- hosting/storage
- phone/email tools
- contractor setup time
- support time
- any paid tools

Founder should know:

```text
How many Premium customers pay for the monthly operating costs?
```

## Financial Discipline

Do:

- collect setup fees once founder pilots prove value
- sell Premium as default
- keep Elite service scoped
- ask for annual prepay after trust exists
- watch support time per customer

Do not:

- discount forever
- promise custom work for cheap
- spend on ads before manual sales works
- give away heavy onboarding without a reason
- let AI usage become invisible cost
