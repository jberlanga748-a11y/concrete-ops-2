# Apex HQ Business Portfolio

## Company Overview

Apex HQ is a contractor growth and operations platform built from field-first contractor workflows.

It combines:

- operations software
- field documentation
- estimating/proposal support
- lead/growth systems
- AI assistant workflows
- owner/admin visibility

## Business Positioning

Apex HQ is for contractors who are good at the work but tired of running the company through scattered texts, spreadsheets, paper notes, phone photos, and memory.

The business promise:

- get organized without becoming corporate
- look more professional without adding office chaos
- keep the field and office connected
- prove the work better
- follow up faster
- prepare the company to grow

## One-Sentence Pitch

Apex HQ gives contractors one command center for leads, estimates, jobs, crews, field proof, reports, and follow-ups.

## What Apex HQ Is Not

- not just a CRM
- not just a proposal app
- not just a photo app
- not just a scheduling app
- not an accounting/payroll replacement today
- not enterprise-ready until the build and safety work prove it
- not an AI autopilot that sends, prices, promises, or spends without approval

## Product Summary

Apex HQ helps contractors manage:

- leads
- customers
- estimates and proposals
- jobs
- crews
- time
- daily reports
- photos/evidence
- delivery tickets
- safety
- checklists
- change orders
- notifications
- growth follow-up

## Service Offerings

Potential offerings:

- software subscription
- managed setup
- website lead intake setup
- proposal/estimate setup
- contractor growth support
- AI-assisted lead/job finding later
- premium onboarding
- monthly growth partner service for Elite customers
- template and process cleanup packages
- owner/admin training
- crew rollout support

## Market Opportunity

Contractors often use scattered tools: texts, spreadsheets, paper forms, photo apps, accounting systems, and generic CRMs. Apex HQ can win by combining field execution, office control, proof-of-work, proposals, and growth support in one practical platform.

## Revenue Model

Primary revenue:

- monthly software subscriptions
- annual subscription prepay
- setup/onboarding fees

Secondary revenue:

- proposal/template setup
- website lead intake setup
- contractor growth support
- monthly business review service
- training and rollout support

Do not depend on paid ads, automatic lead generation, or enterprise contracts for the first stage.

## Expected Cost Watchpoints

| Cost Area | Watchpoint | Practical Control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI/API usage | rough notes, assistant workflows, future lead research can become expensive | usage limits, review-first flows, package limits |
| Support | early customers may need hands-on setup | sell setup fees, keep pilot workflow narrow |
| Infrastructure | more workspaces, uploads, photos, logs, and backups increase cost | monitor storage, upload limits, backup policy |
| Founder time | demos, setup, support, and product direction compete | weekly time blocks and no custom builds during pilots |
| Outreach | paid ads too early can waste cash | manual founder-led outreach first |

## Strengths

- built from real contractor workflow knowledge
- combines field and office
- strong permissions direction
- pilot-grade working modules already exist
- concrete workflows are deeper than generic CRMs
- AI direction is approval-first

## Weaknesses

- not public-SaaS-ready yet
- company isolation and signup need hardening
- billing/packages not implemented
- invite/reset flows need maturity
- UI still needs controlled polish
- support/customer success system needs structure

## Product Roadmap Ideas To Park

These are good business directions but should stay parked until the app/build chat confirms readiness:

- customer portal
- payroll
- accounting/payment integrations
- automatic SMS/email sending
- ad publishing
- AI lead/job finder automation
- offline/PWA field mode
- enterprise SSO/SCIM/SLA commitments

## Long-Term Vision

Apex HQ becomes the contractor operating layer for small teams, growing trade contractors, and larger operators that need field-to-office control plus growth support.
