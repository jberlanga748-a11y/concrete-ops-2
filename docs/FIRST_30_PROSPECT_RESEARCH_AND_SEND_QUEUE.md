## Fit Rules

Best fit:

- owner-led contractor
- 1-5 crews or trying to grow
- active leads, estimates, jobs, photos, field handoff, or follow-up pain
- public website or Facebook presence
- visible services and local service area
- likely using texts, paper, Wix/Facebook, spreadsheets, photos, or manual follow-up

Poor fit:

- huge company with enterprise procurement
- only wants guaranteed leads
- needs full accounting/payroll replacement
- not reachable through public business channels
- requires custom build before testing one workflow
