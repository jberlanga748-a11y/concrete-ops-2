# Apex HQ Sales And Demo Playbook

## Sales Rule

Lead with the contractor's pain. Do not lead with software features.

The demo should feel like:

"Here is how your estimate, job, crew, photos, reports, and follow-ups stop getting scattered."

## Current Demo Position

Apex HQ is close to guided-demo ready and should be sold as a founder-led contractor operations pilot.

For the current production rehearsal script, use `docs/APEX_HQ_FOUNDER_DEMO_RUNBOOK_V618.md`.

Use:

```text
Apex HQ helps contractors keep leads, estimates, jobs, crews, photos, reports, tickets, safety items, and follow-ups organized in one operating system, so owners know what needs attention and field crews know exactly what to do.
```

Avoid:

- public self-serve SaaS language
- guaranteed jobs
- AI autopilot claims
- enterprise readiness claims
- accounting/payroll replacement claims

## Founder Credibility Opener

Use this early, then move back to their pain:

```text
I have spent 15 years in concrete, from being the field guy to running the business side. Apex HQ is being built around the stuff I have seen get lost between the estimate, the job, the crew, the photos, and getting paid.
```

## Discovery Questions

- Where do leads come from today?
- How do you track estimates that need follow-up?
- What gets lost between estimate approval and the crew starting?
- Where do job photos live?
- Who turns in daily reports, tickets, or proof?
- What delays billing or creates arguments?
- What does the owner have to chase every week?
- If Apex HQ only fixed one thing first, what should it be?

## 15-Minute Demo Script

Minute 0-2: Set the frame.

"I will keep this practical. I am going to show how Apex HQ keeps a contractor's leads, estimates, jobs, crews, photos, reports, and follow-ups from getting scattered between the office and field."

Minute 2-4: Show the pain.

"Most contractors have the lead in one place, estimate in another, photos on someone's phone, and the owner chasing updates. Apex HQ puts that work in one command center."

Minute 4-7: Show lead/customer/estimate.

- open a lead or customer
- show basic estimate/job context
- point out follow-up and organization

Minute 7-10: Show job and field proof.

- show job details
- show schedule / today's work
- show crew/field view
- show photos/report/proof
- show delivery ticket, safety, pre-pour/post-pour, or tool checklist only if relevant

Minute 10-12: Show owner/admin review.

- show what is missing
- show what is ready
- explain cleaner billing/proof direction without overclaiming

Minute 12-15: Close for pilot.

"Where would this save you the most chasing: estimates, job handoff, photos, reports, or follow-ups?"

"If we set up one real workflow and tested it for two weeks, would that be worth doing?"

## 30-Minute Demo Script

Minute 0-5: Discovery.

- ask what is scattered
- ask where money is lost
- ask what the owner chases

Minute 5-10: Owner/admin view.

- command center concept
- leads/customers
- estimates/jobs
- follow-ups and status

Minute 10-15: Estimate/proposal flow.

- rough job notes
- estimate/proposal direction
- photos/takeoff backup where available
- field handoff direction

Minute 15-20: Field workflow.

- job details
- crew assignment
- photo upload
- daily report
- tickets/checklists/safety where relevant

Minute 20-25: Review and proof.

- owner reviews what happened
- missing proof gets visible
- job is easier to bill or defend

Minute 25-30: Pilot close.

- confirm best workflow
- confirm users
- agree on kickoff
- set day-3 and day-10 check-ins

## Owner/Admin Demo Flow

Show:

- leads and customers
- estimates and jobs
- crew/job visibility
- proof and reports
- follow-up tracking
- owner review

Say:

"This is built so the owner is not the only person carrying the whole job in their head."

## Foreman/Mobile Demo Flow

Show:

- today's job
- job notes
- upload photo
- daily report
- ticket/checklist/safety item if relevant

Say:

"The field side should be simple. Your crew should see what they need for the job, not office pricing, admin settings, or customer pipeline."

## Proposal/Estimate Demo Flow

Show:

- customer/project details
- estimate line items or estimate context
- photos/attachments direction
- options/GC packet direction if available
- conversion/handoff direction

Say:

"The goal is not fancy paperwork. The goal is a cleaner estimate that turns into a cleaner job handoff."

## Objections And Answers

| Objection | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| We already use spreadsheets. | Keep what works. Apex HQ is for the parts spreadsheets do poorly: field proof, job handoff, photos, reports, and follow-ups in one place. |
| My crew will not use software. | That is why the pilot starts with one simple field action, like photo upload or daily report. If the crew cannot use it, we need to know early. |
| We use Buildertrend/Jobber/etc. | If that is working, great. Apex HQ may still fit if your field proof, estimates, or contractor-specific workflows are scattered. |
| I do not want another monthly bill. | Fair. The pilot should prove whether it saves enough chasing, missed follow-ups, or payment risk to justify itself. |
| Does it replace QuickBooks? | No. Apex HQ is not an accounting replacement right now. It helps organize the work and proof before billing. |
| Does AI send messages for us? | No automatic sending. Any AI direction should be draft/review-first. You stay in control. |
| Is it ready for a large company? | It is pilot-grade and growing. For larger companies, we would start with one narrow workflow before talking about wider rollout. |
| Is this live now? | The app is live and health checks are passing, but we are not opening it as public self-serve yet. We are doing guided demos and controlled pilots first. |
| Will it get me jobs? | The goal is to help you get and win more work by finding opportunities, following up faster, and making cleaner estimates. It is not a guaranteed lead service. |
| What if I am solo? | Then we keep it simple. We would focus on leads, estimates/proposals, job info, website lead flow, and AI help with rough notes, not the full crew package. |

## Closing Questions

- Which workflow would you want to test first?
- Who needs to use it for the pilot to be real?
- What job could we test this on?
- What would make the pilot worth continuing?
- If it works, who else on your team needs to see it?
- Are you comfortable setting up a kickoff this week?

## Pilot Close Script

"Based on what you told me, I would not try to switch your whole company at once. I would set up one workflow: estimate to job to field proof. We test it for 14 days, check in after day 3, and decide at the end if it actually saves you chasing. If it does, we keep you on founder pricing. If it does not, we learn and part clean."
