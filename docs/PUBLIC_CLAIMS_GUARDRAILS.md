# Apex HQ Public Claims Guardrails

Status: active business guardrail
Use for: website, social posts, outreach, demo scripts, pricing conversations, and founder-led sales

## Current Public Position

Use:

```text
Apex HQ is a contractor operations platform entering founder-led demos and controlled pilots.
```

Plain version:

```text
Apex HQ helps contractors keep leads, estimates, jobs, crews, photos, reports, tickets, safety items, and follow-ups organized in one place.
```

## Safe Public Promises

These are acceptable:

- helps organize contractor work
- helps keep leads, estimates, jobs, photos, reports, and follow-ups in one place
- built from 15 years of concrete field and business experience
- founder-led demos are opening
- controlled pilots are available for good-fit contractors
- AI help is review-first where available
- the first pilot focuses on one workflow
- the goal is to reduce chasing and scattered information

## Use With Care

These are allowed only with careful wording:

| Claim | Safer Wording |
| --- | --- |
| get more jobs | help contractors find opportunities, follow up faster, and win more of the work they already come across |
| get paid faster | help organize proof, reports, and job details so billing/review can move cleaner |
| AI helps run the business | AI can help with rough notes and organization where verified, with human review |
| contractor growth platform | contractor operations platform with a growth direction |
| business intelligence | owner visibility and workflow reporting direction |

## Do Not Say Publicly Yet

Do not say:

- guaranteed leads
- guaranteed jobs
- guaranteed revenue growth
- AI runs your business
- AI bids, prices, sends, or approves automatically
- fully automated outreach
- public self-serve SaaS
- enterprise-ready
- replaces Procore, ServiceTitan, QuickBooks, payroll, or accounting
- no setup required
- customers already use it unless true and permissioned
- Apex HQ LLC or Inc. unless the legal entity exists
- any testimonial, case study, logo, revenue, partnership, or customer count that is not real and approved

## Public Website Rule

The website should lead with:

1. who it is for
2. what workflow it organizes
3. founder-led demo CTA
4. controlled pilot language

Do not lead with:

- AI-first language
- guaranteed growth
- broad enterprise claims
- public signup language

## Outreach Rule

Every outreach message should include:

- one practical pain
- one small ask
- no fake familiarity
- no guaranteed result
- no pressure

Best ask:

```text
Would a quick guided walkthrough be worth looking at?
```

## Demo Rule

Show one workflow first:

```text
lead/estimate -> job setup -> field handoff -> photo/report proof -> owner review -> follow-up
```

Do not show every feature unless the contractor asks and the area is clean.

## Competitor Rule

Do not say Apex HQ is better than a competitor.

Say:

```text
Apex HQ is focused on smaller contractor workflows where leads, estimates, job handoff, field proof, and follow-up are scattered.
```

Before publishing competitor comparisons, refresh current competitor pages and separate facts from opinion.

## Compliance Reminder

Cold email/text outreach should stay small, manual, and tracked until the compliance process is tighter.

For cold email, keep:

- truthful subject/from line
- clear identity
- physical mailing address before broad campaigns
- opt-out handling
- no deceptive claims

For text outreach, avoid mass texting and use warm/manual outreach first unless consent rules and tools are handled.

## Approval Rule

Do not publish, send, post, or run ads without John approving the exact message.
