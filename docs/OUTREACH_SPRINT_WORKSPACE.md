## General Outreach Positioning

Use plain contractor language:

- "less chasing"
- "one place for estimates, jobs, photos, reports, and follow-ups"
- "office and field on the same page"
- "not a lead service"
- "not asking you to switch everything"
- "founder pilot"

Avoid:

- guaranteed leads
- enterprise claims
- automatic AI claims
- "revolutionary"
- "all-in-one ecosystem" in first-touch outreach
- long feature lists

## Call Script

Opener:

"Hey, this is John with Apex HQ. I saw [company] does [trade/work type] and I am reaching out because we are opening a small founder pilot for contractors who want estimates, jobs, crews, photos, reports, and follow-ups in one place. Did I catch you at a terrible time?"

If they ask what it is:

"It is a contractor command center. The goal is to reduce the text/spreadsheet shuffle between the office and field. We are starting with practical workflows like estimate-to-job handoff, field photos, reports, and follow-ups."

Qualifying questions:

- "How are you tracking estimates and follow-ups today?"
- "Where do job photos and daily notes usually end up?"
- "Do you have crews in the field using phones now?"
- "What does the owner have to chase the most?"

Close:

"I am not asking you to switch everything. Could I show you a 20-minute walkthrough and, if it fits, we can talk about testing one real workflow?"
