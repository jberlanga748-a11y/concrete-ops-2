# Apex HQ Week 1 Execution Plan

Status: operational founder plan
Owner: John
Use with: `DAILY_CALL_NOTE_INTAKE.md`, `FOLLOW_UP_DRAFT_BANK.md`, `DAILY_OUTREACH_CALL_SHEET.md`, `OUTREACH_LAUNCH_PACKET.md`

## Week 1 Goal

Start real market contact and book the first Apex HQ walkthroughs.

The goal is not to close everyone. The goal is to create proof:

- Which contractors will talk?
- Which pain gets attention?
- Which message books demos?
- Which objections repeat?
- Which companies are worth founder pilot time?

## Week 1 Targets

| Metric | Target |
| --- | ---: |
| New targets reviewed | 50 |
| Calls placed | 25 |
| Emails/texts sent | 25 |
| Real conversations | 5 |
| Demos booked | 2 |
| Pilots offered | 0-1 |
| Notes pasted to Codex | daily |

## Non-Negotiables

- Do not promise features that are not ready.
- Do not sell Apex HQ as fully automated.
- Do not offer custom builds on the first call.
- Do not argue with a no.
- Do not leave notes in your head.
- Every real conversation gets logged the same day.
- Every good conversation gets one next step.
- Every no gets a clean status.

## Primary Offer

```text
I am opening a small Apex HQ founder pilot for contractors who want estimates, jobs, crews, photos, reports, and follow-ups easier to keep track of.

The cleanest pilot is one real workflow for 14 days, usually estimate-to-job or job-to-field-proof.
```

## Primary CTA

```text
Would it be worth a 20-minute walkthrough?
```

## Week 1 Daily Rhythm

### Day 1 - Build The First Live Call Block

Goal: make the first real touches and learn which opener works.

Actions:

- Review 10 high-fit targets.
- Choose the top 5 to call first.
- Send 5 personalized emails or texts after reviewing each company.
- Make 5 calls.
- Leave voicemail only when appropriate.
- Log every touch immediately.
- Paste notes to Codex at end of day.

Call focus:

- Concrete contractors, GC/concrete hybrid shops, hardscape/pavers, excavation/concrete, and owner-led contractors.
- Use trade and city in the first line.
- Keep the call about one practical workflow.

End-of-day output:

- 10 targets reviewed.
- 5 calls completed.
- 5 emails/texts sent.
- 1 strongest opener identified.
- 1 objection list started.

### Day 2 - Follow Up Fast And Book Time

Goal: turn any interest from Day 1 into a scheduled walkthrough.

Actions:

- Review 10 more targets.
- Call 5 new targets.
- Follow up with Day 1 contacts who said "send info" or sounded interested.
- Send 5 personalized emails/texts.
- Offer two demo time options to any warm lead.
- Paste notes to Codex before stopping.

Best follow-up angle:

```text
I can keep it practical and just show the estimate/job/field proof flow. If it does not fit, you will know quickly.
```

End-of-day output:

- 20 total targets reviewed.
- 10 total calls.
- 10 total emails/texts.
- At least 1 demo ask made directly.
- Follow-up dates set for every warm contact.

### Day 3 - Partner And Referral Layer

Goal: add supplier/material yard and contractor referral conversations without slowing direct outreach.

Actions:

- Review 10 more contractor targets.
- Identify 3 referral sources: supplier, material yard, trade contact, GC, estimator, or project manager.
- Make 5 contractor calls.
- Send 5 personalized emails/texts.
- Send 2 light referral asks to people who may know contractors.
- Follow up with all Day 1 non-replies once.
- Paste notes to Codex.

Referral ask:

```text
Do you know one contractor who is constantly chasing estimates, job info, photos, or follow-ups? I am looking for a few practical walkthroughs, not a mass launch.
```

End-of-day output:

- 30 total targets reviewed.
- 15 total calls.
- 15 total emails/texts.
- 3 referral sources touched.
- First pattern summary ready for Codex.

### Day 4 - Demo Booking Push

Goal: convert warm conversations into scheduled walkthroughs.

Actions:

- Review 10 more targets.
- Make 5 calls.
- Send 5 personalized emails/texts.
- Re-contact every warm contact with a specific time option.
- Prepare demo environment and sample workflow before any booked demo.
- Paste notes to Codex.

Use direct scheduling language:

```text
I have [time option 1] or [time option 2]. Would either work for a 20-minute walkthrough?
```

End-of-day output:

- 40 total targets reviewed.
- 20 total calls.
- 20 total emails/texts.
- 1 to 2 demos booked or clear reasons why not.
- Demo prep checklist reviewed.

### Day 5 - Close The Week Strong

Goal: hit Week 1 activity targets and clean the pipeline.

Actions:

- Review 10 final targets.
- Make 5 calls.
- Send 5 personalized emails/texts.
- Follow up with all warm Week 1 leads.
- Mark cold/non-fit leads clearly.
- Ask every good conversation for one referral.
- Paste full week notes to Codex.

End-of-day output:

- 50 total targets reviewed.
- 25 total calls.
- 25 total emails/texts.
- 5 conversations pursued.
- 2 demos booked if possible.
- Clean tracker status for every lead.

### Day 6 - Cleanup, Content, And Proof Assets

Goal: convert the week's learning into usable sales assets.

Actions:

- No cold blitz unless a warm follow-up is scheduled.
- Review notes and update statuses.
- Draft one short founder post from the week's learning.
- Create one demo improvement list.
- Create one objection list.
- Confirm next week's demo times.
- Ask Codex to summarize patterns from all pasted notes.

Codex prompt:

```text
Use this week's Apex HQ call notes to summarize:
1. strongest pains
2. strongest objections
3. best-fit contractor types
4. follow-ups due next week
5. script changes for Week 2
6. demo/pilot opportunities
```

### Day 7 - Weekly Review And Week 2 Prep

Goal: prepare Week 2 without drifting back into planning-only mode.

Actions:

- Review Week 1 scorecard.
- Choose the best 25 to 50 targets for Week 2.
- Confirm all booked demos.
- Prepare custom demo notes for each booked contractor.
- Decide which opener to keep.
- Decide which follow-up draft needs improvement.
- Rest enough to make good calls next week.

## Demo Booking Rules

Book a walkthrough when at least one is true:

- They mention chasing estimates, follow-ups, photos, reports, or job info.
- They ask how Apex HQ works.
- They ask price.
- They use spreadsheets, texts, paper, or memory for active work.
- They say their current system works but still admit something gets messy.
- They ask to "send info" but the call felt warm.

Do not book a walkthrough when:

- They are openly hostile.
- They ask for a feature Apex HQ cannot honestly show.
- They only want leads.
- They want a custom build before seeing the product.
- They ask not to be contacted.
