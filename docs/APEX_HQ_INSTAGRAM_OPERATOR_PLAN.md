# Apex HQ Instagram Operator Plan

Status: draft operating plan
Account: `@apexhqapp`
Rule: do not post, DM, comment, or publish without John's approval.

## Account Position

Apex HQ is a contractor command center for trade businesses.

It helps organize:

- leads
- estimates
- jobs
- crews
- photos
- reports
- change orders
- follow-ups
- owner review

## Posting Rules

Do:

- speak like a contractor
- show messy workflow problems
- explain one practical idea at a time
- use founder-led language
- say built by a contractor
- use all-contractor/trade-business positioning

Do not:

- claim guaranteed leads
- claim guaranteed revenue
- claim fake customers or testimonials
- claim AI runs everything
- claim accounting/payroll replacement
- post customer names/logos without approval
- publish private screenshots/data

## Approval Workflow

1. Agent drafts post.
2. John reviews wording.
3. John confirms if any app screenshot/customer info is safe.
4. John approves post.
5. John posts manually unless a future approved connector exists.
6. Log post date, topic, and response.

## Weekly Content Pillars

1. Contractor workflow pain
2. Office-to-field handoff
3. Leads and follow-up
4. Estimates and jobs
5. Photos/proof/reports
6. Founder story
7. Early access / walkthrough invitation
