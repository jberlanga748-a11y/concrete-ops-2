# Apex HQ Demo Video Playbook

Status: business/marketing draft
Use for: first demo video, outreach follow-up, warm contractor conversations, and founder pilot prep

## Recommendation

Yes, make a demo video.

Do not make a long product tour. Make a short controlled walkthrough that shows the contractor problem and one clean workflow.

Best first video length:

- 2 to 4 minutes for outreach
- 6 to 8 minutes for serious interested contractors
- no 20-minute recording yet

## Why A Demo Video Helps

A short video lets Apex HQ:

- explain the product before a live call
- avoid clicking through confusing parts too early
- show only the strongest guided workflow
- build trust with warm contractors
- give prospects something to rewatch
- reduce pressure on the first live demo
- help John sound clear and confident before calls

## Video Positioning

Say:

```text
Apex HQ is a contractor operations platform entering a controlled founder-led demo and pilot phase.
```

Say:

```text
The goal is to help contractors keep leads, estimates, jobs, crews, photos, reports, tickets, safety items, and follow-ups organized in one place.
```

Do not say:

- fully launched public SaaS
- guaranteed leads
- AI runs your business
- replaces QuickBooks
- replaces payroll
- sends texts or emails automatically
- enterprise-ready

## Best First Video Title

Use one of these:

- `Apex HQ Contractor Operations Demo`
- `Apex HQ Founder Demo: From Lead To Job Proof`
- `Apex HQ Demo For Contractors`

Avoid:

- `Apex HQ Full Product Tour`
- `Apex HQ AI Lead Machine`
- `Apex HQ Launch Video`
- `Apex HQ Software For Everyone`

## First Video Goal

The viewer should understand:

1. What Apex HQ is.
2. Who it is for.
3. What problem it solves.
4. What workflow it helps with.
5. Why the demo/pilot is controlled.
6. What to do next.

They do not need to understand every feature.

## 3-Minute Outreach Video Structure

### 0:00-0:20 - Plain Opener

```text
Hey, this is John with Apex HQ. I have spent 15 years in concrete, from field work to running the business side. I am building Apex HQ for contractors who are tired of leads, estimates, job notes, photos, reports, and follow-ups getting scattered everywhere.
```

### 0:20-0:45 - Problem

```text
Most contractors do not lose work because they are bad at the trade. They lose time because the business side gets spread across texts, phone calls, Facebook, Wix, notebooks, spreadsheets, and pictures on different phones.
```

### 0:45-1:10 - What Apex HQ Is

```text
Apex HQ is a contractor operating system. The goal is to keep leads, estimates, jobs, crews, photos, reports, tickets, safety items, and follow-ups organized in one place so the owner knows what needs attention and the field knows what to do.
```

### 1:10-2:20 - Show One Workflow

Show only this path:

1. Command Center
2. Lead or customer
3. Estimate or job
4. Schedule / today's work
5. Field view
6. Photo/report/proof
7. Owner review / follow-up

Voiceover:

```text
The simple workflow is this: a lead or estimate comes in, it turns into a job, the field sees what they need, photos and reports go back into the job, and the owner can review what is missing or ready instead of chasing everything down.
```

### 2:20-2:45 - AI Carefully

```text
There is also AI help being built in, but it is review-first. It can help with rough notes, organizing information, and finding opportunities, but it does not send, price, approve, or promise anything without a human reviewing it.
```

### 2:45-3:15 - Controlled Pilot CTA

```text
Apex HQ is not open as public self-serve yet. I am doing founder-led demos with a small group of contractors first. If it looks useful, we pick one real workflow and test it before talking about anything bigger.
```

End with:

```text
If you want to take a look, reach out and I can show you the guided demo.
```

## 6-8 Minute Serious Prospect Video

Use this after someone is interested.

Sections:

1. Founder story: 45 seconds
2. Problem: 60 seconds
3. Command Center: 60 seconds
4. Lead/customer/estimate: 90 seconds
5. Job/schedule/field view: 90 seconds
6. Photos/reports/tickets/safety proof: 90 seconds
7. AI review-first explanation: 45 seconds
8. Founder pilot offer: 45 seconds

Do not show:

- deep settings
- billing/package admin
- unfinished areas
- production customer data
- confusing setup pages
- anything that looks like automatic sending

## Screen Recording Checklist

Before recording:

- use demo data only
- close personal tabs
- hide bookmarks if messy
- clear notifications
- zoom browser to a readable size
- use one demo company
- use one demo job
- use one demo field user
- test the path before recording
- write down the 7 clicks you will make

During recording:

- speak slower than normal
- do not apologize for every rough edge
- do not explain every button
- do not click around randomly
- stay on the one workflow
- pause for 1 second before switching pages
- keep the mouse still when talking

After recording:

- watch it once
- cut dead time if easy
- make sure no private data shows
- make sure no unsupported promise was made
- save it as an unlisted/private link first

## Builder Chat Prep Request

Give builder chat this request:

```text
Please prepare Apex HQ for a short controlled demo video. Do not redesign the app. The demo path should be:

Command Center -> Lead/Customer -> Estimate or Job -> Schedule/Today's Work -> Field View -> Photo/Report/Proof -> Owner Review/Follow-up.

Use clean demo data only. Hide or avoid unfinished/confusing areas. Make sure owner/admin and field role boundaries are clear. The goal is a 3-4 minute founder-led video, not a full product tour.
```

## Text To Send With Demo Video

Warm contractor:

```text
I made a short Apex HQ walkthrough so you can see what I mean without jumping straight into a live demo. It shows the basic workflow: lead/estimate to job, field update, photo/report proof, and owner review.

It is still a founder-led pilot, not public self-serve yet. Take a look when you get a minute and tell me if that workflow would actually help you.
```

Cold contractor:

```text
I made a short Apex HQ walkthrough for contractors. It shows how leads, estimates, jobs, crews, photos, reports, and follow-ups can stay organized in one place.

I am showing it to a small group first before opening it wider. If it looks useful, I would be happy to walk through it with you.
```

M2 Mini:

```text
I am going to make a short walkthrough so you can see the simple version without me trying to explain everything over text.

For where you are at being solo, I would focus it on leads, estimates/proposals, job info, website lead flow, and AI help with rough notes. I will send it over once I have it cleaned up.
```

## What The Video Should Not Do

- do not ask them to buy immediately
- do not show every module
- do not make Apex HQ look bigger than it is
- do not promise leads
- do not make AI sound automatic
- do not show confusing or unfinished setup areas
- do not mention public signup
