Status: scope protection for business and builder chats

Status note, 2026-05-17:
Use `docs/APEX_HQ_BUILD_STATUS_AND_PHASES.md` as the current source of truth for completed phases and next work. This file protects scope, but it may lag behind newly released foundations.

## Purpose

This list protects Apex HQ from chasing every idea before the first workflow is proven.

If a contractor asks for something here, log it, but do not promise it.

## Core Rule

First prove:

```text
lead/estimate -> job setup -> field handoff -> photo/report proof -> owner review -> follow-up
```

## Do Not Build Yet

### Full Public Self-Serve Signup

Wait until onboarding, support, demo/real separation, and package enforcement are ready.

### Automated Email/Text Sending

Wait until drafts, approvals, opt-out handling, sender reputation, and compliance are clear.

### AI Autopilot

Do not build or promise:

- AI sending
- AI pricing
- AI approving
- AI bidding without review
- AI calling customers

Allowed direction:

- AI rough notes
- draft help
- organization help
- review-first suggestions

### Accounting / Payroll Replacement

Do not build yet.

Stay focused on job proof, billing readiness, reports, and change notes.

### Deep Integrations

Do not chase QuickBooks, payroll, supplier systems, or complex syncs until pilots prove demand.

### Custom Contractor Builds

Do not promise custom builds in Basic or Premium.

Response:

```text
That is good feedback. I want to test the core workflow first before promising custom work.
```

### Enterprise Features

Do not prioritize SSO, SCIM, enterprise SLAs, procurement workflows, or advanced audit/legal controls until Apex HQ has smaller contractor traction.

### Paid Ads

Do not run broad paid ads until the demo converts manually and first pilot proof exists.
