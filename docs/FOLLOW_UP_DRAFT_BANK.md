## Rules For Every Follow-Up

- Keep it short.
- Reference the real pain or trade.
- Ask for one clear next step.
- Do not promise full automation.
- Do not promise lead generation.
- Do not offer custom builds.
- Do not pressure a no.
- If they ask to stop, stop.
