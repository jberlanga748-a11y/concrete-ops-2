# Apex HQ 90-Day Content Calendar

Status: founder-led content plan

## Content Rule

Post practical contractor lessons, not hype.

Best content comes from:

- real field pain
- founder concrete experience
- demo objections
- pilot lessons
- checklists
- before/after workflow examples

## Weekly Content Rhythm

| Day | Content Type |
| --- | --- |
| Monday | contractor pain / question |
| Tuesday | quick workflow tip |
| Wednesday | founder field lesson |
| Thursday | proof/report/follow-up checklist |
| Friday | founder pilot invite or lesson learned |
| Saturday | longer practical post |
| Sunday | weekly reflection / next week focus |

## Month 1 Themes

- job details in texts
- missing photos
- estimate follow-up
- field proof
- owner chasing crews
- concrete field lessons
- why one workflow is better than switching everything

Post examples:

```text
Contractors: what gets lost most often: estimate follow-ups, job photos, crew updates, daily reports, or change notes?
```

```text
After 15 years in concrete, one thing is obvious: if the job proof is not easy to find, it might as well not exist when there is a dispute.
```

```text
You do not need to switch your whole company to software overnight. Start with one workflow: job setup, field photo, report, owner review.
```

## Month 2 Themes

- first pilot lessons
- cleaner handoffs
- owner/admin visibility
- crew adoption
- customer/GC communication
- proposal and proof quality

Post examples:

```text
The best contractor software workflow is the one the field will actually use.
```

```text
A clean estimate-to-job handoff should answer: what are we doing, where, when, who is going, what proof is needed, and what follow-up is due?
```

## Month 3 Themes

- case studies if real
- pricing/value lessons
- referral asks
- partner/supplier lessons
- premium workflow direction
- growth without chaos

Post examples:

```text
Growth breaks messy systems. If the owner is the only person who knows where everything is, adding more jobs just adds more chasing.
```

```text
Good contractors lose time when job details are scattered. Apex HQ is being built to clean that up from estimate to field proof.
```

## Channels

Use:

- LinkedIn
- Facebook contractor/local groups
- personal business profile
- website blog later
- email follow-up snippets

Avoid:

- daily sales pitch
- fake case studies
- customer data without permission
- overclaiming product readiness
