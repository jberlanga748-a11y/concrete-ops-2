## Guardrails

- Do not send, post, DM, or text without John's approval.
- Do not claim guaranteed leads, guaranteed revenue, or guaranteed growth.
- Do not claim AI autopilot.
- Do not claim payroll/accounting replacement.
- Do not claim customer proof unless it exists and permission is documented.
- Keep Apex HQ positioned for all contractors and trade businesses, not concrete-only or fence-only.
- Important actions stay review-first: sending, pricing, billing, payroll, customer communication, and record changes need owner review.

## Compliance Notes

This is business-risk guidance, not legal advice. Before scaling cold outreach, confirm the exact rules with a qualified professional or compliant sending platform.

- Cold texts should be used carefully. Use manual one-to-one messages first, include identity, keep the message relevant, and honor opt-outs immediately.
- Cold text wording should include permission-safe language such as "Reply STOP and I will not text again."
- Commercial email should use truthful sender/header information, honest subject lines, a real business identity, a physical mailing address when used as commercial email at scale, and a clear opt-out path.
- Do not keep following up after someone asks not to be contacted.

## Recommended First 30 Send Sequence

Do not blast all channels at once. Use one primary touch, then follow up manually.

| Day | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Day 1 | Verify 10 prospects. Send 5 texts and 5 emails manually. |
| Day 2 | Call the 10 prospects from Day 1. Leave voicemail if no answer. Verify 10 more. |
| Day 3 | Send Follow-Up Email 1 or 2 to non-responders. Send 5 more texts and 5 more emails. |
| Day 4 | Call Day 3 prospects. Log every reply. |
| Day 5 | Send no-hype follow-up to warm/qualified non-responders. Stop anyone who opts out. |
| Day 6 | Ask warm contacts for referrals. |
| Day 7 | Review replies, objections, demos booked, and best message angle. |
