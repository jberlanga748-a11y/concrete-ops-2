# Apex HQ Partner Outreach Playbook

Status: working partner development system
Owner: John Berlanga
Use: manual outreach only

## Purpose

Build a practical local partner channel that introduces Apex HQ to contractors through people who already have trust with them: suppliers, rental yards, GCs, subcontractor networks, bookkeepers, insurance/bonding contacts, and trade/local groups.

This is not a paid partner program yet. The first goal is conversations, referrals, demos, and learning.

## Positioning

Apex HQ helps contractors keep estimates, jobs, crews, photos, reports, and follow-ups organized instead of scattered across texts, memory, spreadsheets, and disconnected files.

Use John's 15 years of concrete experience as trust, not as a guarantee:

```text
I have spent 15 years in concrete, from field work to the business side. Apex HQ is being built from the problems contractors actually deal with: estimates, job details, crew updates, photos, reports, and follow-up getting scattered everywhere.
```

Short version:

```text
I am building Apex HQ from 15 years of concrete experience, and I am looking for a few honest contractor conversations.
```

## Partner Principles

- Ask for introductions, not favors that create pressure.
- Keep partner requests specific and easy.
- Do not promise referral fees until the business model and tracking are clean.
- Do not imply a partner endorses Apex HQ unless they explicitly agree.
- Do not use fake partner names in sales material.
- Track every conversation in `docs/PARTNER_TARGET_TRACKER.md`.
- Thank people quickly, even when they do not have a referral.

## Best Partner Categories

| Category | Why They Matter | Best Ask | Best First Offer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Material yards | Know active concrete contractors and hear job chaos firsthand | "Who is busy and could use cleaner job tracking?" | Short walkthrough and founder pilot invite |
| Concrete suppliers | Know volume, reliability, scheduling pain, and growing crews | "Who is doing good work but buried in follow-up?" | Practical contractor workflow review |
| Equipment rental yards | See active jobs, growing contractors, and scheduling pressure | "Who is renting more often and starting to outgrow texts?" | 20-minute demo focused on job organization |
| GCs | Need subs to communicate clearly and provide job proof | "Which subs are good but need cleaner updates?" | Show how Apex HQ can help subs send cleaner updates |
| Subcontractor networks | Contractors trust peer referrals more than software ads | "Who do you know that is busy and needs a better system?" | Founder pilot conversation |
| Bookkeepers/accountants | See messy billing, missing job info, and owner overload | "Which contractor clients are organized enough to grow but still buried?" | Workflow conversation around job proof and ready-to-bill tracking |
| Insurance/bonding contacts | Know contractors trying to look more professional and reliable | "Who is growing and needs better documentation habits?" | Operations polish conversation |
| Trade/local groups | Gather contractors in one trusted place | "Can I share a practical workflow checklist?" | Educational talk, checklist, or founder pilot invite |

```text
I am not asking you to sell anything. I am looking for a few contractors who would give honest feedback on a 20-minute walkthrough and maybe test a small founder pilot.
```


## Referral Ask By Category

### Material Yard / Concrete Supplier

```text
Who is ordering regularly, staying busy, and starting to look like they need a better way to track estimates, crews, photos, and follow-up?
```

### Equipment Rental Yard

```text
Who is renting more often, juggling multiple jobs, or growing past what they can keep straight in texts?
```

### GC

```text
Which subs do good work but could look more professional with cleaner updates, job photos, reports, and follow-up?
```

### Subcontractor / Peer Contractor

```text
Who do you know that is good in the field but tired of chasing estimate notes, job photos, crew updates, and customer follow-up?
```

### Bookkeeper / Accountant

```text
Which contractor clients have enough work to grow but still struggle with missing job details, billing backup, or follow-up tracking?
```

### Insurance / Bonding

```text
Who is trying to become more organized and professional as they grow, especially around documentation and job records?
```

### Trade / Local Group

```text
Would it be useful if I shared a practical contractor workflow checklist or gave a short talk on keeping job photos, reports, and follow-ups organized?
```

## Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
| --- | --- |
| "I do not want to sell software." | "I am not asking you to sell it. I am only looking for one or two contractors who may give honest feedback." |
| "Our customers are busy." | "That is exactly why I want the ask to be light: a 20-minute walkthrough only if it seems useful." |
| "Is this a CRM?" | "Partly, but contractor-specific. The focus is estimates, jobs, field proof, reports, crews, and follow-up in one place." |
| "Do you pay referrals?" | "Eventually we may build a formal referral program. Right now I am keeping it simple and founder-led until tracking and economics are clean." |
| "What if they already use software?" | "That is fine. I am looking for feedback from real contractors, not asking anyone to rip out their current system." |
| "Who else is using it?" | "The pilot is early and intentionally small. I am looking for honest contractor feedback before making bigger claims." |
