# Apex HQ Pricing And Package Strategy

Status: planning recommendation. Billing and package enforcement should not be treated as fully launched until the app build chat confirms readiness.

## Pricing Principles

- Sell value, not feature count.
- Keep setup practical and founder-led at first.
- Do not make security, permissions, company isolation, or safe data handling premium features.
- Do not promise AI autopilot, automatic outreach, accounting replacement, payroll, or enterprise readiness before they are built and verified.
- Use pilot pricing to learn willingness to pay, onboarding effort, support load, and which workflows create value fastest.

## Recommended Public Packages

### Basic

Target customer:

- owner-operator or small contractor
- 1 to 2 crews
- wants simple organization without heavy systems
- needs leads, customers, estimates, jobs, crews, photos, reports, and follow-ups in one place

Included:

- leads and customers
- simple estimates
- jobs
- crews
- uploads/photos
- daily reports
- basic field visibility
- basic safety/checklist workflows where available
- notifications/reminders foundations where available
- demo-to-real guided setup
- standard support

Excluded:

- advanced proposal/GC packet workflows
- advanced AI assistant workflows
- Opportunity Scout / lead-finding
- advanced analytics
- advanced integrations
- managed growth services
- customer portal
- enterprise controls

Monthly price range:

- $99 to $199 per month

Pilot pricing:

- $49 to $99 per month for first 3 months after pilot
- or $500 setup plus $99 per month if setup effort is significant

Annual idea:

- 2 months free on annual
- example: $990 to $1,990 per year

Setup/onboarding fee:

- $250 to $750, waived or discounted for founding pilots

Upsell when:

- they need better proposals
- they have more than one active crew
- they want field proof tied to billing readiness
- they ask for reporting, growth help, or better estimate packets

### Premium

Target customer:

- growing contractor with multiple jobs, crews, or an office/admin person
- needs cleaner estimate-to-job handoff
- wants more professional proposals and field documentation
- has enough job volume that missing photos, follow-ups, and change orders cost real money

Included:

- everything in Basic
- enhanced proposal/estimate workflow where available
- branded proposal/packet direction
- advanced field proof workflow
- change order tracking
- review/approval workflow direction
- app health/watchtower style reminders when available
- advanced reporting direction
- limited AI rough notes and assistant workflows where verified
- priority onboarding

Excluded:

- done-for-you website/ads/growth partner services
- automatic outbound email/SMS
- full customer portal until built
- deep accounting/payroll replacement
- enterprise SSO/SCIM/SLA commitments

Monthly price range:

- $249 to $499 per month

Pilot pricing:

- $149 to $249 per month for first 3 months
- or $1,000 setup plus $249 per month

Annual idea:

- 10% to 15% discount or setup fee waived
- example: $2,700 to $5,100 per year depending on seats/setup

Setup/onboarding fee:

- $750 to $2,500 depending on services, templates, users, and workflow depth

Upsell when:

- owner wants more leads or better close rate
- company needs estimate/proposal polish
- they are asking for growth help, website help, or repeat reporting
- they are outgrowing manual follow-up

### Elite

Target customer:

- serious growth-minded contractor
- multiple crews or divisions
- owner wants Apex HQ plus hands-on business/growth support
- values setup, process design, templates, and recurring optimization

Included:

- everything in Premium
- premium onboarding and workflow mapping
- proposal/template setup
- owner/admin training
- crew rollout support
- monthly business review
- growth partner service direction
- website lead intake setup where available
- content/outreach planning support
- advanced AI/growth tools only as they become verified and approved

Excluded:

- guaranteed leads or revenue
- ad spend
- automatic ad publishing
- automatic outreach without approval
- accounting/payroll replacement
- enterprise security commitments unless separately scoped and built

Monthly price range:

- $799 to $1,500 per month software plus services
- higher custom service packages only after value is proven

Pilot pricing:

- $499 to $799 per month for 3 months after setup
- or a fixed 60-day growth setup package

Annual idea:

- annual contract with quarterly business reviews
- setup fee reduced when annual is prepaid

Setup/onboarding fee:

- $2,500 to $7,500 depending on migration, templates, users, training, website/lead intake, and process support

Upsell when:

- owner asks for help getting more work
- they want a better website/intake/follow-up process
- they need repeat accountability, reporting, and team adoption
- they have enough revenue that one saved job dispute or won job pays for the service

## Founder Pilot Offer

Recommended offer:

- 14-day pilot
- no long-term contract during pilot
- one owner/admin user plus selected field users
- setup help included
- one real workflow selected before kickoff
- day-3 check-in
- day-10 value review
- end-of-pilot close call

Current selling posture:

- guided demos can start now
- paid pilots should stay narrow and founder-led
- public self-serve SaaS pricing should wait until onboarding, support, and package enforcement are ready

Pilot close options:

- Basic founder plan: $99/month for 6 months
- Premium founder plan: $249/month for 6 months
- Elite founder plan: $750/month for 3 months with hands-on setup

Founding customer benefit:

- locked founder pricing window
- direct founder support
- influence on workflow priorities
- early testimonial opportunity

Founder requirement:

- use Apex HQ on one real workflow
- provide honest feedback
- allow anonymous lessons to shape product
- optional testimonial only if value is real

## Pricing Recommendation

Start by selling Premium as the main plan.

Basic should exist so small contractors have an entry point, but it should not become the main sales motion. Elite should be sold selectively when the customer clearly wants services and has enough value at stake.

Recommended default close:

"For the founder group, I would start you on Premium at $249/month after the pilot. I will help set up your first workflow so we can see if it actually saves you chasing and keeps the office and field cleaner."

Solo contractor close:

"For where you are at right now, I would not put you on the big package. I would start you on Basic founder pricing at $99/month, no long-term contract, focused on leads, estimates/proposals, job info, website lead flow, and AI help with rough notes."
