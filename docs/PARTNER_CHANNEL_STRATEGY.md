# Apex HQ Partner And Channel Strategy

Status: business development plan

## Goal

Use local trust channels to reach contractors without relying on ads too early.

Best early partners:

- concrete/material suppliers
- equipment rental yards
- local GCs
- subcontractor networks
- trade associations
- local business groups
- bookkeepers/accountants serving contractors
- insurance/bonding contacts
- website/marketing freelancers serving trades

## Partner Message

```text
Apex HQ helps contractors keep estimates, jobs, crews, photos, reports, and follow-ups organized. I am opening a small founder pilot and looking for contractors who are good at the work but buried in job details.
```

## Material Yard / Supplier Strategy

Why they matter:

- they know which contractors are busy
- they hear complaints about missed orders, job chaos, and scheduling
- they have trusted local relationships

Ask:

```text
Who do you know that does good work but is buried in estimates, job photos, crew updates, or follow-up?
```

Offer:

- no pressure
- short walkthrough
- founder pilot
- referral credit later if appropriate

## Equipment Rental Strategy

Best angle:

- contractors with equipment needs usually have active jobs
- job scheduling, crew handoff, and proof matter

Ask:

```text
Which small contractors around here are growing fast and starting to need better job organization?
```

## GC/Subcontractor Strategy

For GCs:

```text
Apex HQ may help subs give cleaner job updates, photos, reports, and follow-up.
```

For subcontractors:

```text
Apex HQ helps you look more professional with better job proof and cleaner follow-up.
```

Avoid:

- saying Apex HQ guarantees GC work
- pretending GC portal is live if it is not
- promising enterprise workflows

## Trade Association Strategy

Offer practical education, not a sales pitch.

Topic ideas:

- "How contractors stop losing job photos and follow-ups"
- "Simple field proof habits that protect payment"
- "How to clean up estimate-to-job handoff"
- "What to track before a job is ready to bill"

CTA:

```text
I am opening a small founder pilot for contractors who want help setting up this workflow.
```

## Facebook Group Strategy

Rules:

- do not spam
- answer real questions
- share checklists
- ask practical questions
- move interested people to DMs only when appropriate

Post idea:

```text
Contractors: what gets lost the most in your business right now: estimate follow-ups, job photos, crew updates, daily reports, or change order notes?
```

## LinkedIn Strategy

Weekly posts:

- contractor workflow pain
- field proof tips
- before/after process examples
- founder pilot openings
- lessons from demos without naming companies

Direct message:

```text
Saw your work at [company]. I am opening a small founder pilot for Apex HQ, focused on helping contractors keep estimates, jobs, photos, reports, and follow-ups organized. Worth a 20-minute walkthrough?
```

## Referral Partner Offer

Start simple:

- thank-you referral credit
- one free month for customer referrals
- setup credit
- later: partner fee only if economics and legal/payment handling are clean

Do not create complicated partner payouts before first sales are proven.
