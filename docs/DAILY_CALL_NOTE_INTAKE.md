Status: operational note template
Owner: John

## Purpose

This file keeps daily outreach notes clean enough for Codex to turn them into follow-ups, tracker updates, demo prep, and weekly pattern summaries.

The rule: capture rough notes fast, then paste them to Codex once per day in the same structure.

## Interest Level Guide

Low:

- Short answers.
- No clear pain.
- No next step.
- Asked only to get off the phone.

Medium:

- Mentioned one real pain.
- Asked a question.
- Said to send info.
- Might look later.

High:

- Explained their current system.
- Mentioned a repeated problem.
- Asked how Apex HQ works.
- Asked price.
- Accepted a walkthrough time.
- Gave a referral or second contact.

## Codex Request Menu

Use one of these requests after pasting notes.

### For Follow-Ups

```text
Use these Apex HQ notes to draft follow-up emails/texts for each warm or medium-interest contact. Keep John's voice direct, practical, and honest. Do not overpromise automation, lead generation, or custom features.
```

### For Tracker Cleanup

```text
Turn these notes into a tracker-ready table with company, contact, status, interest level, pain, objection, next action, follow-up due date, demo date, and notes.
```

### For Demo Prep

```text
Based on these notes, prepare a 20-minute Apex HQ walkthrough plan for each demo. Include discovery questions, best workflow to show, likely objections, and the clean close.
```

### For Weekly Pattern Review

```text
Summarize all pasted Apex HQ outreach notes into: best segment, strongest pain, best opener, common objections, demo candidates, pilot candidates, referral opportunities, and script changes for next week.
```

## What To Capture Word-For-Word

Write exact language when they say anything about:

- what they are tired of chasing
- why they do or do not use software
- what they currently pay for
- how they handle estimates
- how field photos/reports are collected
- why they would test Apex HQ
- why they would not test Apex HQ
- what price feels fair or too high

## Daily Closeout Checklist

- Every contacted lead has a status.
- Every warm lead has a next action.
- Every follow-up has a due date.
- Every objection is logged.
- Every demo has a date/time or a clear scheduling next step.
- Every do-not-contact request is marked clearly.
- Notes are pasted to Codex before the day ends.
