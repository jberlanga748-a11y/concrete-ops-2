# Apex HQ Aggressive 18-Month Execution Plan

Status: founder execution plan
Assumption: founder is willing to work 12-hour days, 7 days/week
Goal: reach a credible path to $1M+ ARR run-rate as fast as possible without faking readiness

## North Star

Build Apex HQ into a serious contractor operations and growth company.

18-month aggressive target:

- 150 to 300 paying customers
- $50K to $90K MRR
- strong shot at $1M ARR run-rate
- Premium validated as the main package
- Elite service accounts funding growth
- repeatable sales/onboarding/retention system

## Non-Negotiable Rule

Hours do not matter unless they create business proof.

Every day should create at least one of:

- contractor conversation
- demo booked
- demo completed
- pilot started
- customer converted
- referral collected
- testimonial/case study
- retention save
- pricing insight
- product insight from a real customer

## Phase 1: Days 1-30 - Get In The Market

Goal:

- stop planning in isolation
- talk to contractors every day
- book demos
- learn the strongest pain

Targets:

- 100 contractor contacts
- 50 calls
- 20 replies/conversations
- 8 to 12 demos booked
- 5 demos completed
- 2 to 3 pilots offered
- 1 to 2 pilots started

Daily work:

- call 5 to 10 contractors
- send 5 to 10 personalized emails/texts
- update tracker
- improve scripts from real objections
- prep next day call sheet

Do not:

- redesign the app
- build custom requests
- overthink branding
- buy ads
- promise features not ready

## Phase 2: Days 31-90 - Convert Pilots

Goal:

- prove contractors will pay
- prove one workflow creates value
- collect testimonials/referrals

Targets:

- 200 to 300 total contacts
- 20 to 30 demos completed
- 10 pilots started
- 3 to 10 paid customers
- 2 testimonials or case studies
- 15 to 20 referral names

Main focus:

- 14-day pilot process
- day-3 check-ins
- day-10 value reviews
- Premium pricing reaction
- onboarding friction
- crew adoption

Pass criteria:

- at least 30% of qualified pilots convert
- Premium is accepted by at least one real customer
- at least one customer can explain the value in their own words

## Phase 3: Months 4-6 - Build Repeatability

Goal:

- stop relying only on brute force
- make the sales/onboarding motion repeatable

Targets:

- 20 to 40 paid customers
- $5K to $12K MRR
- 3 to 5 testimonials/case studies
- 50+ referral names collected
- 2 partner channels producing leads

Main focus:

- repeatable demo
- repeatable onboarding
- referral motion
- material yard/supplier outreach
- contractor group content
- first annual offers
- setup fees

Founder schedule:

- mornings: outbound/calls
- mid-day: demos/follow-ups
- afternoon: onboarding/support
- evening: content, partner work, review

## Phase 4: Months 7-12 - Scale The Working Motion

Goal:

- turn founder sales into a system
- reduce onboarding/support drag
- push MRR toward $20K-$45K

Targets:

- 75 to 150 paid customers
- $20K to $45K MRR
- churn tracked monthly
- 10 case studies/testimonials
- 5 active referral/partner sources
- first support/sales contractor considered

Main focus:

- customer success process
- retention health score
- partner/channel strategy
- content from real lessons
- case studies
- annual plans
- selective automation with approval

Hiring/help trigger:

- if founder spends more than 25% of week on repeat support/admin, get help
- if demos are available but not enough time to run them, get sales/admin help
- if onboarding blocks new sales, get customer success help

## Phase 5: Months 13-18 - Push Toward $1M Run-Rate

Goal:

- reach $50K-$90K MRR
- turn Apex HQ into a real operating company

Targets:

- 150 to 300 paid customers
- $50K to $90K MRR
- Premium is main revenue base
- Elite accounts add high-margin service revenue
- support process is not founder-dependent
- acquisition channel is measurable

Main focus:

- sales process
- customer success system
- retention and expansion
- partner program
- annual contracts
- selected integrations/automation only if product team confirms readiness
- stronger brand/category

## Daily 12-Hour Operating Rhythm

| Time Block | Focus | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1.5 hr | planning + call prep | call sheet, priorities |
| 3 hr | outbound calls/messages | conversations, demos |
| 2 hr | demos/follow-ups | booked pilots, recaps |
| 2 hr | onboarding/customer success | pilot progress, retention |
| 1 hr | partner/channel work | referral sources |
| 1 hr | content/proof assets | post, case study, demo clip idea |
| 1 hr | tracker/metrics/admin | clean pipeline |
| 0.5 hr | daily review | tomorrow plan |

## Decision Gates

After 30 days:

- Which trade responded best?
- Which pain got the most interest?
- Which message booked demos?

After 90 days:

- Are contractors paying?
- Is Premium realistic?
- What workflow converts?
- What breaks onboarding?

After 6 months:

- Is acquisition repeatable?
- Is churn manageable?
- Does support scale?
- Is Apex HQ ready for selective automation?

After 12 months:

- Is there a path to $1M ARR with current pricing?
- Does the business need hiring, funding, or partner scale?
- Which package should dominate?

## Burnout Guardrail

Aggressive does not mean sloppy.

Do not let 12-hour days turn into:

- random feature chasing
- bad promises
- ignored support
- no sleep decisions
- discounting from panic
- selling custom work as product

Protect:

- sleep
- clear notes
- weekly review
- honest product claims
- customer trust
