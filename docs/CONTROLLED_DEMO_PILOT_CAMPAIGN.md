# Apex HQ Controlled Demo And Pilot Campaign

Status: active business draft
Use for: first guided demos, founder pilot conversations, outreach, and follow-up

## Current Readiness Position

Apex HQ is close to guided-demo ready and usable for founder-led walkthroughs.

Market it as:

```text
Apex HQ is a contractor operations platform entering a controlled founder-led demo and pilot phase.
```

Do not market it as:

- public self-serve SaaS
- fully automated lead generation
- an enterprise construction platform
- an accounting/payroll replacement
- a finished website/marketing agency
- AI that runs the company without human review

## Core Demo Message

```text
Apex HQ helps contractors keep leads, estimates, jobs, crews, photos, reports, tickets, safety items, and follow-ups organized in one operating system, so owners know what needs attention and field crews know exactly what to do.
```

## Best First Audience

Target 5 to 10 contractors first.

Best fits:

- owner-led contractor
- concrete, excavation, hardscape, remodel, fencing/siding/framing, small GC, or specialty trade
- one owner still carrying too much in their head
- lead follow-up happens through phone, Facebook, Wix, email, texts, or spreadsheets
- estimates are handwritten, loosely tracked, or hard to follow up
- job photos, reports, tickets, and field proof are scattered
- company wants to grow but does not have a clean operating system yet

Avoid for first wave:

- contractors demanding a full custom build
- companies needing enterprise procurement
- anyone asking for guaranteed leads
- companies that need accounting/payroll replacement immediately
- contractors who will not use even one real workflow

## Campaign Goal

The first campaign is not about mass outreach.

The goal is:

1. Book 5 to 10 guided walkthroughs.
2. Learn what contractors understand or find confusing.
3. Identify 2 to 3 paid pilot candidates.
4. Set up one real workflow per pilot.
5. Collect feedback, objections, and plain-language proof.

## The Offer

```text
I am opening a small founder pilot for contractors. I am not trying to switch your whole company overnight. I want to show you the workflow, pick one part that could help, and see if it is worth testing for 14 days.
```

## Pricing Direction For Demos

Use pricing only after interest is real.

Solo / Basic founder pilot:

- $99/month
- best for owner-operator or solo contractor
- no long-term contract during founder pilot
- focus: leads, estimates/proposals, job info, website lead flow, rough notes, simple AI help

Growing contractor / Premium founder pilot:

- $249/month
- best for 1 to 5 crews or an owner/admin managing multiple jobs
- focus: estimate-to-job handoff, field proof, reports, tickets, follow-ups, crew visibility, AI rough notes

Hands-on growth / Elite founder pilot:

- $750+/month
- only if they want hands-on setup, templates, training, website lead flow, and growth support
- do not offer first unless the contractor has clear value at stake

## What To Say About AI

```text
The AI side is there to help you move faster, not to make promises for you. It can help find and sort opportunities, turn rough notes into cleaner drafts, and help organize the office side. You still review everything before anything goes out or becomes real.
```

Do not say:

- AI guarantees jobs
- AI bids jobs for you automatically
- AI sends emails or texts without review
- AI replaces the owner

## 15-Minute Guided Walkthrough

Minute 0-2: Frame it.

```text
I will keep this practical. I am going to show how Apex HQ keeps the business side from getting scattered between leads, estimates, jobs, crews, photos, reports, and follow-ups.
```

Minute 2-4: Ask two questions.

- Where do your leads come from right now?
- What is the messiest part: estimates, job handoff, field updates, photos, or follow-up?

Minute 4-7: Show owner/admin command.

- dashboard / command center
- leads
- customers
- estimates
- jobs
- reminders / notifications

Minute 7-10: Show job-to-field flow.

- job details
- schedule / today's work
- assigned crew or field user
- field visibility without office/pricing access

Minute 10-12: Show field proof.

- daily report
- uploads/photo evidence
- delivery tickets
- pre-pour / post-pour if relevant
- safety / incidents / checklist if relevant

Minute 12-14: Show why it matters.

```text
The point is the owner does not have to chase every update, and the field does not have to guess what matters. The job has one place to live.
```

Minute 14-15: Close.

```text
If we tested this, I would not start with everything. I would pick one real workflow and see if it actually helps.
```

## Founder Pilot Close

```text
The cleanest way to test Apex HQ is one real workflow for 14 days. We set up the workflow, you use it on real work, we check in after day 3, and then we decide if it is worth continuing.
```

If they ask price:

```text
It depends on company size. Solo contractors would likely start around $99/month during the founder pilot. Growing contractors with crews would likely be closer to $249/month. I would rather match the price to what you would actually use instead of overselling you.
```

## Testimonial Plan

Do not ask for a testimonial before value is real.

After a useful walkthrough:

```text
What part looked most useful for your company?
```

After a useful pilot:

```text
Can I use one sentence from you about what Apex HQ helped clean up? Nothing hype. Just what changed.
```

Good testimonial themes:

- easier to track estimates
- photos/report proof easier to find
- less owner chasing
- cleaner job handoff
- field knew what to do
- follow-up did not get buried

## Daily Campaign Targets

Founder-led pace:

- 5 warm texts/calls per day
- 5 researched cold targets per day
- 2 follow-ups per day
- 1 demo or pilot conversation per day if possible
- update tracker before ending the day

## Biggest Risks

- showing too much and confusing the contractor
- overpromising AI or leads
- pricing too low for companies with crews
- demoing before choosing the contractor's pain
- turning every conversation into a custom feature request
- calling it public SaaS before onboarding and safety are fully ready

## Best Next Move

Start with M2 Mini and 4 to 9 other warm or local contractors.

For each contractor:

1. Confirm their current workflow.
2. Ask what they want to improve.
3. Match them to Basic, Premium, or later.
4. Offer a short guided demo only when the demo path is clean.
5. Close for one workflow, not the whole company.
