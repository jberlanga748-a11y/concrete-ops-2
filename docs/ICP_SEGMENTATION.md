# Apex HQ ICP Segmentation

Status: target customer definition

## Best First ICP

Owner-led concrete, hardscape, excavation, and small GC companies with active field crews and scattered office-to-field communication.

Best company size:

- 2 to 25 field workers
- 1 to 5 crews
- owner/admin still involved in estimating, scheduling, job tracking, and follow-up

Best pain:

- estimates need follow-up
- job details live in texts
- photos are stuck on phones
- field reports are inconsistent
- owner chases crews
- change order proof is weak
- work is ready to bill but proof is scattered

## ICP Tier 1: Concrete Contractor

Why it matters:

- founder has 15 years concrete experience
- concrete jobs need proof, photos, tickets, reports, and clean handoff
- strong niche credibility

Best message:

```text
Built from 15 years of concrete field and business experience to help contractors keep estimates, jobs, crews, photos, reports, and follow-ups organized.
```

Main pilot workflow:

- estimate -> job -> crew -> photos/report -> owner review

## ICP Tier 2: Hardscape / Exterior Project Contractor

Why it matters:

- photo proof and customer communication matter
- projects have stages and visual progress
- often owner-led and under-systemized

Best message:

```text
Keep estimates, job photos, crew notes, schedules, and customer follow-ups from getting buried in texts.
```

## ICP Tier 3: Excavation / Sitework Contractor

Why it matters:

- field conditions change quickly
- equipment, crew, photos, and job notes matter
- proof helps with customers, GCs, and changes

Best message:

```text
Keep job notes, field proof, crew updates, and follow-ups organized from office to field.
```

## ICP Tier 4: Small GC / Remodeler

Why it matters:

- more varied jobs
- estimates, subs, photos, customer updates, and follow-ups scatter easily

Best message:

```text
One place for project notes, estimates, job photos, reports, and follow-ups.
```

## Bad Fit For Now

- company only wants payroll/accounting
- company requires enterprise procurement before pilot
- crew refuses any phone-based workflow
- owner wants custom software before testing core workflow
- company wants guaranteed leads
- company wants AI autopilot to send/price/approve automatically

## Qualification Score

Score 5 if:

- active jobs
- field crews
- owner/admin pain
- scattered photos/reports/follow-ups
- willing to test 14-day pilot

Score 3 if:

- good trade fit but pain unclear
- may need education
- not urgent

Score 1 if:

- no clear workflow pain
- wants unrelated features
- poor timing
- not willing to try pilot
