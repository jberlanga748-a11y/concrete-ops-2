# Apex HQ Monthly Expense Model

Status: planning estimate, update with real bills monthly
Use for: cash planning, pricing decisions, founder pay, and reinvestment

## Important

This is not accounting advice. It is a planning model.

Actual costs depend on the final hosting setup, database usage, AI/API usage, email volume, support load, and tools selected.

## Current Expense Principle

Until Apex HQ has paid pilots, keep monthly expenses as low as possible.

Do not add subscriptions unless they directly help:

- keep the app running
- book demos
- onboard pilots
- support customers
- track money and taxes
- capture proof

## AI/API Cost Watchpoint

AI can become one of the biggest variable expenses.

Rules:

- do not offer unlimited AI usage early
- keep human review
- use caps per plan
- track AI cost per customer
- track AI cost per feature
- do not sell low-priced plans with heavy AI workload

Basic should not include unlimited AI.

Premium can include controlled AI assistance.

Elite can justify heavier AI/support only if priced correctly.

## Support Cost Watchpoint

Founder time is a cost even if no cash leaves the bank.

Track:

- support time per customer
- onboarding time per pilot
- number of calls per customer
- repeated questions
- bugs blocking workflow

If a $99/month customer takes 5 hours of support every month, the plan is not profitable.

## Infrastructure Cost Watchpoint

Watch:

- uploads/photos
- report files
- AI requests
- background jobs
- email/SMS volume
- database size
- app traffic

Do not let photo/upload-heavy customers stay on underpriced plans without limits.

## Cash Rules

1. Keep monthly expenses under $150 until the first paid pilot unless a cost directly unlocks demos.
2. Keep monthly expenses under $400 until at least $1,000 MRR.
3. Do not add paid ads until manual demos convert.
4. Do not add automation tools until manual workflow proves demand.
5. Do not include unlimited AI in low-price plans.
6. Charge setup fees when onboarding requires real time.
7. Review expenses every month before founder pay.

## Pricing Implication

Basic at $99/month only works if support and AI usage stay low.

Premium at $249/month should become the main plan because it gives enough room for:

- hosting
- AI/API use
- support time
- onboarding
- reinvestment
- profit

Elite exists because some contractors will need hands-on setup and growth help. That cannot be sold at Basic pricing.
