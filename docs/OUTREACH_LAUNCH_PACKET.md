## Outreach Rules

- Personalize the first line with trade and city.
- Keep first email under 130 words when possible.
- Do not attach heavy files.
- Do not send more than one message per channel per day.
- If they say no, mark `Not a Fit` or `Do Not Contact`.
- If they reply with interest, stop sequences and move to demo booking.
- If they ask for features not ready, answer honestly and park roadmap ideas.
