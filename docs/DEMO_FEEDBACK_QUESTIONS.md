# Apex HQ Demo Feedback Questions

Status: ready-to-use draft
Use for: every guided walkthrough and demo recap

## Demo Feedback Goal

The goal is not to hear "looks cool."

The goal is to learn:

- what the contractor understood
- what confused them
- which workflow felt valuable
- whether they would pay
- what price range makes sense
- whether they are a pilot fit

## Before The Demo

Ask:

```text
Before I show anything, what are you using right now to track leads, estimates, jobs, photos, and follow-ups?
```

```text
What gets most annoying when you get busy?
```

```text
Are you mostly solo, or do you have employees/crews?
```

```text
If one thing got cleaned up, what would matter most?
```

Also ask when needed:

```text
What happens between estimate approval and job setup?
```

```text
How does the crew get job details, notes, photos, or changes?
```

```text
Where do job photos, daily notes, issues, or proof of work usually end up?
```

```text
Do you have unsold estimates that never get followed up on?
```

## During The Demo

Ask after the first workflow:

```text
Does this match how work actually moves for you, or would your company do it different?
```

Ask when showing estimates/proposals:

```text
Would this be cleaner than how you are writing estimates now?
```

Ask when showing jobs/field:

```text
Would this help you or your field guys know what needs done without chasing texts?
```

Ask when showing photos/reports:

```text
Would having photos and reports tied to the job save you time later?
```

Ask when showing AI:

```text
Would AI help with rough notes or finding opportunities be useful if you still review everything first?
```

## After The Demo

Ask:

```text
What part made the most sense?
```

```text
What part felt confusing or too much?
```

```text
Where would this actually save you time?
```

```text
What would you use first: leads, estimates, jobs, field updates, photos/reports, or follow-up?
```

```text
Would this be more useful for you as a solo owner, an admin tool, or a crew/job tool?
```

```text
If we tested this for 14 days, what real workflow should we test?
```

```text
Would you pay to keep that workflow if it worked?
```

## Price Reaction Questions

Only ask after they see value.

Basic:

```text
For a solo contractor version around $99/month, would that feel fair if it helped with leads, estimates, job info, website lead flow, and rough notes?
```

Premium:

```text
For a growing contractor with crews, would $249/month feel fair if it helped with estimate-to-job handoff, field updates, photos, reports, and follow-ups?
```

Elite:

```text
If someone wanted hands-on setup, templates, training, and monthly growth support, that would be a different package. Is that something you would even care about, or would you rather keep it simple?
```

## Demo Scorecard

Score each demo 1 to 5.

| Category | Score | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Understood the product | 1-5 |  |
| Has real workflow pain | 1-5 |  |
| Correct company size | 1-5 |  |
| Willing to test one workflow | 1-5 |  |
| Price reaction positive | 1-5 |  |
| Support burden looks manageable | 1-5 |  |

Pilot-ready score: 24+.

Follow-up only: 18-23.

Not a fit right now: under 18.

## Simple Five-Part Fit Score

Use this if you are on a call and need to score fast.

| Category | Strong Signal |
| --- | --- |
| Pain | clear recurring pain costing time, money, or owner attention |
| Workflow fit | strong fit for lead/estimate to job to field to proof to follow-up |
| Decision maker | owner or key decision maker is present and engaged |
| Urgency | wants to solve this soon |
| Pilot readiness | willing to run one real workflow in the next 7 days |

Score 1 to 5 each.

- 21-25: strong pilot fit
- 16-20: possible pilot fit
- 10-15: feedback only
- under 10: not a fit right now

## Objection Log

Record exact words when possible.

| Objection | What It Probably Means | Response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "I already use Wix." | They think Apex HQ is only website/software | "That makes sense. I am not trying to replace the website first. I am looking at what happens after the lead comes in." |
| "Will it get me jobs?" | They want lead generation | "The goal is to help you find opportunities and follow up faster, but I do not want to promise guaranteed jobs." |
| "I do everything myself." | Basic plan fit | "Then I would keep it simple: leads, estimates, job info, and follow-up." |
| "My guys won't use apps." | field adoption risk | "Then I would start with one simple action, like photos or a daily report, not the whole system." |
| "I don't have time to set it up." | onboarding needed | "That is why the pilot is guided. We pick one workflow and set it up together." |

## End Of Demo Close

If good fit:

```text
I would not try to switch your whole company. The cleanest test would be one workflow for 14 days. Based on what you said, I would test [workflow]. If it helps, we keep going. If it does not, we stop or adjust.
```

If not ready:

```text
That helps. I do not think I should push you into a pilot yet. I am going to keep cleaning up the demo and I may check back once it fits your setup better.
```

## What To Send After Demo

```text
Thanks for taking a look at Apex HQ. My notes were:

- Current setup: [current tools]
- Biggest pain: [pain]
- Best workflow to test: [workflow]
- Possible plan: [Basic/Premium/later]

The next step, if you want to test it, would be a 14-day founder pilot around that one workflow.
```

## What Counts As A Win

A demo is a win if at least one happens:

- they explain their workflow clearly
- they point to one useful Apex HQ area
- they ask price
- they agree to another call
- they give a referral
- they tell you what confused them

Do not treat every demo as a sales close. Early demos are market truth.
