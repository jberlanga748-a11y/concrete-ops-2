# Apex HQ Real Objection Bank

Status: working bank

## Rule

Expected objections are useful, but real objections matter more.

Log the exact words contractors use.

## Current Real Objections / Questions

### "How much does it cost per month?"

Response:

```text
For where you are at being solo, I would keep it simple. The Basic founder pilot would be $99/month if the workflow looks useful. No long contract. We would test leads, estimates/proposals, job info, website lead flow, and rough notes first.
```

### "I have something like this through Wix."

Response:

```text
That makes sense. I am not trying to replace your website first. I am looking at what happens after a lead comes in: estimate, follow-up, job info, photos, reports, and keeping everything from getting scattered.
```

### "I do everything myself."

Response:

```text
Then I would keep it simple. You probably do not need the full crew side right now. I would focus on leads, estimates/proposals, job info, website lead flow, rough notes, and follow-up.
```

### "Will it get me jobs?"

Response:

```text
That is the goal, but I do not want to sell it like a lead company promising jobs. Apex HQ is meant to help you find opportunities, follow up faster, make cleaner estimates/proposals, and keep job info organized so you can win more of the work you already come across.
```

## Expected Objections

### "I already use software."

```text
That is fine. I am not asking you to throw out what works. I would only look at where things still get messy: estimate handoff, job photos, reports, field proof, or follow-ups.
```

### "My guys will not use apps."

```text
Then I would not start with the whole crew. I would start with one simple field action, like photo proof or a daily report, and see if it is actually usable.
```

### "Can you build this custom thing?"

```text
I am open to feedback, but I do not want to promise custom builds before the core workflow proves useful. The pilot should test what is ready first.
```
