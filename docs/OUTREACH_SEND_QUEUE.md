## Daily Send Checklist

- business email only
- personalize first line
- confirm company name spelling
- confirm contact channel
- keep CTA to 20-minute walkthrough
- log status immediately
- schedule next follow-up
- do not send automated sequences

## Reply Handling

Interested:

- send demo booking reply
- offer two times
- ask for best calendar email
- update status to `Demo Scheduled`

Not interested:

- thank them
- ask for referral only if conversation was warm
- mark `Not a Fit` or `Do Not Contact`

Already using software:

- ask what still gets messy
- offer walkthrough only if there is a pain fit
- do not argue against their current tool

Asks price:

- explain founder pilot first
- say pricing depends on fit/package
- use `PRICING_PACKAGE_STRATEGY.md` if they are qualified

Asks if features are live:

- be honest
- say Apex HQ is opening a small founder pilot
- do not claim public-SaaS or enterprise readiness
