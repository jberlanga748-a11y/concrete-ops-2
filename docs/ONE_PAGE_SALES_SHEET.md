# Apex HQ One-Page Sales Sheet

Status: send-after-call draft

## Apex HQ

Contractor operations software for owners who are tired of chasing job details.

## What It Is

Apex HQ helps contractors keep leads, estimates, jobs, crews, photos, reports, tickets, safety items, and follow-ups organized in one place.

It is entering founder-led demos and controlled pilots.

## Built For

- concrete contractors
- hardscape contractors
- excavation contractors
- remodelers
- small GCs
- specialty contractors with field crews or job proof needs

Best fit:

- owner-led
- active estimates/jobs
- job info spread across texts, photos, paper, spreadsheets, or memory
- wants cleaner handoff between office and field

## Main Workflow

```text
lead/estimate -> job setup -> field handoff -> photo/report proof -> owner review -> follow-up
```

## Founder Pilot

The pilot does not try to switch your whole company at once.

We pick one real workflow, set it up, test it for 14 days, and decide if it is worth continuing.

Includes:

- guided setup
- one selected workflow
- owner/admin setup
- one field user if needed
- day-3 check-in
- day-10 value review

## Founder Pricing Direction

- Basic founder pilot: around $99/month for solo contractors or very small teams
- Premium founder pilot: around $249/month for contractors with crews and field handoff needs
- Elite founder support: $750+/month only for hands-on setup, templates, training, and growth workflow support

## What Apex HQ Does Not Promise

- guaranteed leads
- guaranteed jobs
- AI autopilot
- accounting/payroll replacement
- full company migration on day one
- custom build before the core workflow proves useful
