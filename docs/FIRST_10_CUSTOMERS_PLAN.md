# Apex HQ First 10 Customers Plan

## Goal

Get 10 real contractor pilots without overclaiming the product, overspending, or creating custom work that distracts from the core platform.

The first 10 customers are not just revenue. They should prove:

- who feels the pain fastest
- which workflow creates day-one value
- what setup help is required
- what pricing feels fair
- what support issues repeat
- what messaging gets replies

## Best First Targets

Start with contractors who are close enough to give honest feedback and simple enough to onboard manually.

Priority order:

1. Owner-led concrete contractors with 1 to 5 crews.
2. Concrete plus excavation/hardscape contractors.
3. Small GCs who self-perform field work.
4. Remodel/hardscape contractors with strong photo and customer communication needs.
5. Larger civil/concrete companies only for feedback unless they are willing to pilot a small workflow.

Avoid for first 10:

- companies demanding enterprise procurement
- companies that require accounting/payroll integration on day one
- companies asking for custom software
- companies with no owner/admin willing to participate

## Where To Find Them

- existing personal contractor network
- Oregon concrete and hardscape websites
- Google Maps
- Facebook contractor and local business groups
- LinkedIn owner/PM searches
- material yards and concrete suppliers
- equipment rental counters
- GCs who hire subs
- subcontractors who complain about field proof and change orders
- trade association directories

## The Offer

Plain version:

"I am opening 10 founder pilot spots for contractors who want leads, estimates, jobs, crews, photos, reports, and follow-ups in one place. I will help set up one real workflow, you test it on a real job, and we decide after two weeks if it is worth continuing."

Pilot terms:

- 14 days
- no long-term contract during pilot
- one workflow selected before kickoff
- owner/admin must attend kickoff and close call
- day-3 check-in required
- no custom feature promises
- no public testimonial unless they choose to give one
- founder pricing available only after pilot decision

## Demo Booking Script

"Hey, I am working with a small group of contractors on Apex HQ. It is built to keep leads, estimates, jobs, crews, photos, reports, and follow-ups from getting scattered across texts and spreadsheets. I am not selling a lead service or asking you to switch everything. Would a 20-minute walkthrough be worth looking at?"

## First Demo Flow

1. Ask what gets scattered today.
2. Show lead/customer.
3. Show estimate.
4. Convert or connect to job.
5. Show crew/job assignment.
6. Show field user view.
7. Show photo/report proof.
8. Show owner review.
9. Ask which part would save the most chasing.
10. Offer a 14-day pilot around that workflow.

## Follow-Up Plan

Day 0:

- send short recap and booking link/times
- confirm trade, crew count, biggest workflow pain

Day 1 after no reply:

- send one-line follow-up with specific pain

Day 3:

- send a practical angle: photos/reports/follow-ups

Day 7:

- soft close: "Should I close the loop?"

After demo:

- same day recap
- pilot fit summary
- setup requirements
- decision date

## Referral Plan

Ask after any useful conversation:

"Who do you know that is good at the work but buried in estimates, texts, photos, or crew follow-up?"

Ask after pilot value is proven:

"Would you be comfortable introducing me to two contractors who run into the same kind of job tracking or field proof headache?"

Referral reward idea:

- one free month for a successful referral
- or setup credit
- or a founder discount extension

## Testimonial Plan

Do not ask for a testimonial until they get a real win.

Good testimonial moments:

- first job set up cleanly
- first field report submitted
- first photo/report helped answer a customer or GC question
- first estimate/proposal looked more professional
- first follow-up was not missed

Ask:

"Can I use one sentence from you about what Apex HQ helped clean up? No hype. Just what changed."

## First 10 Success Metrics

| Metric | Target |
| --- | --- |
