# Apex HQ Risk And Reward Matrix

## Reward Matrix

| Opportunity | Reward | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Field proof-of-work | reduces disputes and speeds payment | High |
| Estimate/proposal quality | helps contractors win more work | High |
| Owner command center | gives owners control without chasing texts | High |
| Watchtower reminders | reduces forgotten tasks | High |
| Website/lead intake | creates growth path | Medium |
| AI rough notes and assistant | saves paperwork time | Medium |
| Package tiers | supports monetization | Medium |
| Enterprise controls | unlocks larger customers | Later |

## SWOT

| Category | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Strengths | contractor-first workflows, office/field connection, proof-of-work direction, concrete pilot niche, approval-first AI stance |
| Weaknesses | pilot-grade readiness, public signup/package enforcement not fully mature, customer success still manual, limited market proof |
| Opportunities | first 10 founder pilots, concrete/hardscape/excavation niche, setup revenue, referral network, supplier/GC relationships |
| Threats | established competitors, contractor resistance to software, support overload, security trust issues, AI cost/expectation creep |

## Risk Matrix

| Risk | Why It Matters | Mitigation | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Company data leakage | destroys trust | company-scope tests and server-side checks | P0 |
| Field role leakage | exposes pricing/admin data | role tests and sanitized bootstrap payloads | P0 |
| Demo/real data mixing | embarrassing and unsafe | separate apps/volumes and demo gates | P0 |
| AI over-action | sends/prices/promises without approval | human approval mode and audit history | P1 |
| Contractor adoption friction | users stop after day one | field-first UX and pilot feedback | P1 |
| Founder overload | too many tracks at once | Master Coordinator and Now/Next/Later control | P1 |
| Billing before value | distracts from adoption | defer billing until pilot workflows prove value | P2 |
| Hidden GPS concern | trust/legal risk | explicit consent/settings only | P1 |
| Integration complexity | fragile support load | add integrations after core workflows stabilize | P2 |
| Overclaiming readiness | hurts trust and creates support pressure | say pilot-grade until verified | P1 |
| Package confusion | makes sales harder | keep Basic/Premium/Elite simple | P2 |
| Cash/credit pressure | can force rushed decisions | sell setup, avoid ad spend, keep pilots focused | P1 |

## Founder Risk

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Too many jobs: sales, setup, support, product, marketing | time-block founder-led sales and support; avoid custom builds |
| Selling ahead of product | use pilot-grade language and one-workflow pilots |
| Weak follow-up discipline | maintain outreach tracker and weekly sales rhythm |
| Personal network fatigue | expand through referrals, suppliers, local groups, and associations |

## SaaS Risk

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Public signup before isolation is proven | keep wider launch gated until build readiness is confirmed |
| Support volume grows faster than revenue | charge setup fees and document repeat onboarding steps |
| Billing/packages become a distraction | sell manually first; implement billing after value is proven |
| Upload/storage growth | define storage limits and monitor costs before scale |

## Contractor Adoption Risk

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Crews refuse to use it | start with one field action, not full rollout |
| Owner does not change habits | schedule day-3 and day-10 check-ins |
| Company expects custom software | keep pilot scope fixed |
| Too much setup friction | founder-led setup and templates |

## AI Risk

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| AI promises too much | position AI as draft/review assistant |
| AI/API costs surprise the business | package limits and usage monitoring |
| AI sends/prices/promises incorrectly | no automatic send, approval, pricing, or schedule promises |

## Support Risk

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Early customers need heavy handholding | charge onboarding/setup after founder pilots |
| Bugs block pilots | pilot narrow workflows and use severity triage |
| Feedback turns into random product requests | classify feedback as sales, onboarding, roadmap, or support |

## Mitigation Rules

- Security and company isolation come before public signup scale.
- Human approval comes before AI automation.
- Pilot workflows come before billing complexity.
- Clear field UX comes before advanced dashboards.
- Release only after focused QA and rollback notes.
- Manual founder-led sales come before paid acquisition.
- Customer proof comes before enterprise claims.
