# Apex HQ First 10 Demo Targets

Status: active target plan
Use for: choosing who gets the first clean guided demos

## Goal

Book 10 guided demo conversations with the right contractors, not random people.

The first 10 demos should answer:

- who understands Apex HQ fastest
- which trade feels the pain most
- which workflow is easiest to sell
- what pricing sounds fair
- who could become the first paid pilot
- what objections keep repeating

## Ideal First Demo Customer

Best fit:

- owner-led contractor
- uses texts, Facebook, Wix, paper, spreadsheets, phone photos, or memory to run work
- actively sends estimates or proposals
- has jobs moving from office to field
- wants to grow but does not have a clean operating system
- owner will give honest feedback

Strong trades:

- concrete
- hardscape
- excavation
- fencing
- siding
- framing
- remodel
- small GC
- specialty subcontractor with field proof needs

Weak first-wave fit:

- wants only guaranteed leads
- refuses to do any admin cleanup
- needs full accounting/payroll integration first
- expects custom software
- too large for founder-led onboarding right now

## Target Tiers

### Tier 1: Warm Known Contractors

These are the highest priority because trust already exists.

Examples:

- M2 Mini
- contractors John has poured near or worked around
- Last Yard Concrete contacts
- local contractors who already know John can do the work

Why they matter:

- fastest reply chance
- most honest feedback
- easiest first walkthrough
- lower trust barrier

### Tier 2: Local Similar Contractors

These are nearby companies with visible activity.

Look for:

- active Facebook page
- basic website or Wix site
- photos of recent jobs
- owner still involved
- phone number visible
- not obviously enterprise-level

### Tier 3: Growing Small Crews

These are better Premium candidates.

Look for:

- multiple crews or multiple job types
- hiring posts
- before/after job photos
- public bid or commercial work mentions
- need for job handoff, photos, reports, and proof

### Tier 4: Larger Companies For Feedback Only

Use them for market learning, not first paid pilots unless they ask for a narrow test.

## Fit Score

Score each prospect 1 to 5.

| Factor | Score |
| --- | --- |
| Owner-led | 1-5 |
| Has active estimates/jobs | 1-5 |
| Workflow looks scattered | 1-5 |
| Trust or local connection | 1-5 |
| Could pay $99-$249/month | 1-5 |
| Can give useful feedback | 1-5 |
| Owner/decision maker is reachable | 1-5 |

Best first demo target: 30+ total score.

Good target after top list: 24-29.

Research more before outreach: 18-23.

Do not chase under 18 unless there is a strong relationship.

## Fit Verification Questions

Ask before demo:

```text
Where do most of your leads come from right now?
```

```text
How are you writing estimates or proposals right now?
```

```text
What gets messier as you get busier: leads, estimates, job info, field updates, photos, or follow-up?
```

```text
Do you have employees or crews, or are you mostly solo right now?
```

```text
If we tested something, what one workflow would actually help?
```

## Day-By-Day First 10 Sequence

### Day 1

- send M2 Mini follow-up
- text 4 warm contractors
- call 3 warm contractors
- research 5 local contractors

### Day 2

- follow up with warm non-replies
- email 5 researched contractors
- call 5 local contractors
- book first 1 to 2 demos

### Day 3

- run first guided demo if demo cleanup is finished
- log objections and confusing points
- ask for 2 referrals from any warm contact

### Day 4

- run or book 2 more demos
- send demo recap to everyone who watched
- identify first pilot candidate

### Day 5

- close one narrow 14-day pilot if fit is real
- send close-the-loop follow-ups
- update first 10 board

## What To Record After Every Conversation

Track:

- company
- owner/contact name
- trade
- crew count
- lead sources
- estimate/proposal method
- current tools
- biggest pain
- how they schedule jobs
- how they hand work to the field
- where photos/videos live
- how they follow up on unsold estimates
- price reaction
- demo booked yes/no
- pilot fit yes/no
- objections
- next follow-up date
- exact words worth saving

## Red Flags

Pause or downgrade a target if they say:

- "Can it guarantee me jobs?"
- "Can it do all my payroll?"
- "Can you build my custom thing first?"
- "I do not want to enter anything anywhere."
- "I need my whole company migrated this week."

## Best First Demo Outcome

The best first 10 demos should produce:

- 2 to 3 paid pilot candidates
- 1 clear Basic solo plan use case
- 1 clear Premium crew workflow use case
- 10+ objections logged
- 2 referral asks
- 1 plain-language testimonial later, only after value is real

## Pilot Readiness Signals

Good signs:

- they describe real workflow pain without being pushed
- they say details are spread across texts, notes, photos, or memory
- they have enough job volume for the problem to matter
- they are willing to spend 20 minutes walking through their process
- they ask practical questions about using it on real work
- they can name one workflow they would test first

Weak signs:

- they only want more leads
- they expect full automation immediately
- they want payroll/accounting replacement
- they will not describe their current process
- they are curious but have no urgent pain
- they want custom features before seeing the core workflow
