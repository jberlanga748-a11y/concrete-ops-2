## Phone Script

```text
Hey, this is John with Apex HQ. I saw [company] does [trade/work type] around [area]. I am reaching out because I am opening a small founder pilot for contractors who want estimates, jobs, crews, photos, reports, and follow-ups easier to keep track of. Did I catch you at a bad time?
```

If they give you time:

```text
Apex HQ is not a lead service, and I am not asking you to switch everything. I am looking for a few contractors to walk through one real workflow, like estimate-to-job handoff or field photos and reports, and tell me if it would actually help.
```

Close:

```text
Would it be worth a 20-minute walkthrough? I can show the workflow, you can tell me where it is useful or where it misses.
```
