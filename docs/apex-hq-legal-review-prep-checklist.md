# Apex HQ Legal Review Prep Checklist

Status: founder/legal review prep, not legal advice

Purpose: organize the open legal, claims, privacy, support, and pilot-contract questions before wider paid launch, public self-serve signup, automated billing, customer portal sharing, integrations, or enterprise/security claims.

Use with:

- `docs/PILOT_TERMS_AND_SUPPORT_POLICY.md`
- `docs/CUSTOMER_DATA_POLICY_DRAFT.md`
- `docs/PUBLIC_CLAIMS_GUARDRAILS.md`
- `docs/LAUNCH_READINESS.md`
- `docs/apex-hq-pilot-readiness-checklist.md`

## Boundary

This checklist does not approve legal language, create terms, create a privacy policy, authorize public launch, authorize paid billing, or replace attorney review.

Before using formal customer-facing terms, privacy language, testimonials, public AI claims, billing terms, or uptime/security commitments, have the final language reviewed by a qualified professional.

## Source References For Review

Use official business guidance as review inputs, not as a substitute for legal advice:

- FTC business privacy/security guidance: `https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/privacy-security`
- FTC small business protection resources: `https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/small-businesses`
- FTC endorsement/testimonial guidance: `https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides`
- FTC endorsements, influencers, and reviews topic page: `https://www.ftc.gov/consumer-protection/endorsements-influencers-reviews`

## Minimum Review Packet

Before a paid pilot or broader launch, assemble:

- current pilot terms/support policy draft
- customer data policy draft
- public claims and demo copy
- pricing/package wording
- support/severity process
- backup/export/offboarding process
- incident/security contact process
- AI feature wording and review-first boundaries
- testimonial, quote, logo, screenshot, and case-study permission process
- customer pilot setup guide and environment-isolation rules

## Terms Review Questions

Confirm:

- pilot length, start date, end date, and end-state options are clear
- founder-led support limits are clear
- no unsupported SLA or uptime commitment is implied
- no custom feature promise is implied
- no payroll, accounting, tax, legal, or compliance replacement claim is implied
- package/pricing language does not imply self-serve billing or automated plan changes before those exist
- cancellation, continuation, data export, and offboarding expectations are clear enough for a pilot

## Privacy And Data Review Questions

Confirm:

- what data Apex HQ collects for a pilot
- what data Apex HQ should not collect during early pilots
- how customer workspaces stay separated
- what field users can and cannot see
- when support may inspect workspace records
- how screenshots, photos, logos, testimonials, and customer examples require permission
- export/offboarding language does not overpromise automation
- incident/security contact path is clear
- AI data-use wording is accurate and conservative

## Public Claims Review Questions

Review every public page, demo packet, outbound script, and sales deck for:

- guaranteed lead/revenue claims
- AI autopilot claims
- enterprise-ready claims
- SOC 2, HIPAA, PCI, bank-level, or compliance claims
- guaranteed uptime/SLA claims
- accounting/payroll replacement claims
- customer portal/self-serve approval claims before those features are production-ready
- automatic email/SMS/customer-contact claims before explicit consent and sending workflows exist

Safe default:

```text
Apex HQ is a founder-led pilot platform for contractor operations workflows, with review-first AI assistance and role-based workspace separation.
```

## Testimonial And Social Proof Review

Before using a quote, logo, screenshot, job photo, customer name, or case study, capture:

- who gave permission
- exact asset or quote approved
- whether anonymous use is required
- where it may be used
- whether the result is typical or a specific customer story
- date permission was given
- whether any payment, discount, referral credit, or material connection exists

Do not use synthetic, AI-generated, paid, discounted, or founder-written testimonials as if they came from real customers.

## AI Claims Review

Confirm all AI wording stays review-first:

- AI may organize, summarize, draft, or prepare information for human review
- AI does not approve estimates, submit bids, contact customers, schedule crews, place orders, or make commitments automatically
- Opportunity Scout remains review-first and does not bypass logins, CAPTCHA, MFA, paywalls, robots.txt, or access controls
- any AI data-use disclosure matches actual system behavior

## Billing And Payment Review

Before adding or promoting paid self-serve billing:

- formal pricing/package terms are reviewed
- payment processor terms are reviewed
- cancellation/refund language is reviewed
- tax/invoice responsibility is clear
- no Stripe, checkout, invoices, or payment collection are implied before implementation exists

## Support And Incident Review

Confirm:

- P0/P1/P2/P3 definitions are plain-language and realistic
- data exposure or role leak concerns route to P0
- support owner is named for pilots
- support response language says "target" or "when practical" unless a formal SLA is approved
- incident notes location and customer notification process are known before active pilots

## Go / No-Go Gate

Do not move from controlled founder-led pilot to wider paid launch until:

- formal terms are reviewed
- privacy/data policy is reviewed
- public claims are reviewed
- testimonial permission process is reviewed
- pilot support boundaries are reviewed
- export/offboarding expectations are reviewed
- AI claims are reviewed
- payment/billing claims are either removed or matched to implemented, reviewed billing workflows

## Current Recommendation

Apex HQ can continue guided demos and controlled founder-led pilots using conservative language and manual review gates.

Wider paid launch remains blocked until formal legal/privacy/claims review is complete.
