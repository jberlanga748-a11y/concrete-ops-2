# Apex HQ Trust And Compliance Checklist

Status: business checklist, not legal advice

## Purpose

Protect trust before scaling outreach, pilots, testimonials, SMS, email, AI, or customer data.

## Outreach Compliance

- use business email only
- no personal Gmail
- identify sender clearly
- do not mislead recipients
- honor opt-out/do-not-contact immediately
- no bulk SMS blasting
- no automatic email/text sending before approval rules exist
- keep outreach factual

## Testimonial Permission

Before using a quote:

- ask permission
- confirm name/company usage
- offer anonymous option
- do not edit meaning
- do not imply results that did not happen
- save permission note

## Customer Data Trust

Do not show:

- real customer data in demos without permission
- another company's workspace
- pricing/margins to field users
- private notes outside intended users

Say:

```text
We take company data separation and role permissions seriously. Wider launch depends on those foundations being ready.
```

Do not say:

- "enterprise-grade security" unless verified
- "fully compliant" unless reviewed
- "bank-level security" unless true and documented

## SMS And Calling

Before agent-assisted texting/calling:

- use business number
- define opt-out process
- log messages
- define human approval
- avoid automated cold SMS until rules are clear
- disclose AI voice if ever used
- provide human fallback

## AI Claims

Allowed:

- review-first assistant direction
- draft support
- organizing notes
- human approval

Avoid:

- AI sends automatically
- AI prices jobs
- AI promises schedules
- AI approves work
- AI replaces owner judgment

## Contract/Terms Readiness

Needed before wider paid launch:

- terms of service
- privacy policy
- acceptable use
- payment terms
- cancellation policy
- data handling summary
- support policy
- testimonial release language

## Launch Gate

Before scaling past founder-led pilots:

- business email configured
- demo data safe
- pilot terms clear
- support intake ready
- customer data claims reviewed
- SMS/email rules reviewed
- package promises match app readiness
