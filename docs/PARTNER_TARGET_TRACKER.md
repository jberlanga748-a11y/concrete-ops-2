# Apex HQ Partner Target Tracker

Status: working partner tracker
Use: update after every partner touch
Rule: categories and placeholders only until real targets are researched and manually entered

## Tracker Rules

- Do not add fake partner names.
- Use placeholders until a real business/contact is identified.
- Log every call, visit, email, text, referral, and intro.
- Ask for permission before using a partner's name publicly.
- Do not promise payment, revenue, or customer volume.
- Mark `Do Not Contact` immediately if requested.
- Record the contractor referral source when a referral becomes a lead.

## Partner Priority Score

Score each target from 1 to 5.

| Score | Meaning |
| ---: | --- |
| 5 | Warm relationship, direct contractor access, likely to refer |
| 4 | Strong category fit, local trust, reachable contact |
| 3 | Useful category, unknown relationship strength |
| 2 | Possible but weak access or unclear fit |
| 1 | Low priority or research only |
