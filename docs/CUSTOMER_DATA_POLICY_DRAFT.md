# Apex HQ Customer Data Policy Draft

Status: draft customer-facing summary, not legal advice
Owner: Apex HQ founder
Use with: `docs/PILOT_TERMS_AND_SUPPORT_POLICY.md`, `docs/TRUST_COMPLIANCE_CHECKLIST.md`, and `docs/PUBLIC_CLAIMS_GUARDRAILS.md`

## Purpose

This draft gives Apex HQ a plain-language customer data handling summary for founder-led demos and controlled pilots.

It is not a substitute for a formal privacy policy, data processing agreement, security policy, or legal review. Formal documents should be prepared before wider paid launch, public self-serve signup, enterprise sales, integrations, automated messaging, or regulated customer requirements.

## Short Customer Summary

Use this in pilot conversations:

```text
Apex HQ is designed to keep each contractor workspace separate, keep field users away from office-only information, and use customer data only to support the workflow being tested. We do not use your company name, screenshots, quotes, or real customer examples publicly without permission.
```

## What Data A Pilot May Include

For a controlled pilot, Apex HQ may store or process:

- contractor company name and contact details
- owner/admin and field user names, roles, email addresses, and phone numbers when provided
- customer names and contact details needed for the pilot workflow
- leads, estimates, jobs, schedules, reports, uploads, delivery tickets, checklists, safety notes, and follow-ups
- support notes and feedback shared during the pilot
- operational activity and audit history used to troubleshoot and review work

Only ask for data needed for the selected pilot workflow.

## What Apex HQ Should Not Collect During Early Pilots

Do not request or store unless a later approved phase requires it:

- payroll data
- bank account data
- tax identifiers
- credit card data
- full accounting exports
- employee background checks
- sensitive personal information unrelated to contractor operations
- hidden GPS/location tracking
- customer portal credentials for external customers

## Workspace Separation

Each contractor should have a separate workspace or approved pilot environment.

Expectations:

- one company should not see another company's data
- support/demo workflows should not mix real customer data with shared demo data
- production demo data should stay synthetic unless the customer gives permission to use their data
- field users should see assigned field work only

Field users must not receive:

- owner/admin settings
- pricing, margins, payroll, or billing data
- leads or estimates unless a later role policy explicitly allows it
- unrelated jobs
- another company workspace

## Use Of Customer Data

Apex HQ may use pilot data to:

- operate the selected workflow
- troubleshoot support issues
- improve onboarding and usability
- review whether the pilot is creating value
- prepare customer-requested exports or summaries when available

Apex HQ should not use customer data to:

- train public marketing claims
- publish case studies
- create testimonials
- contact the contractor's customers
- send automated messages
- share with another contractor
- run ads or public content

without explicit permission and a separate approved process.

## Demo And Screenshot Rules

Do not show real customer data in demos unless permission is clear.

Before using any screenshot, quote, logo, customer name, job photo, or case study externally, capture:

- what will be used
- where it will appear
- whether the company/name can be shown
- whether anonymous use is preferred
- date permission was given

Safe default:

```text
Use synthetic demo data unless the customer explicitly approves otherwise.
```

## Support Access

During founder-led pilots, support may review workspace information only to:

- help the customer use the selected workflow
- troubleshoot a reported issue
- review pilot success criteria
- prepare an agreed follow-up or support summary

Support should not browse customer data casually, use it in unrelated demos, or share it outside the support context.

## Data Exports And Offboarding

For early pilots:

- keep offboarding manual and founder-led
- provide reasonable export/support help when practical
- do not promise enterprise-grade export automation unless it exists and is verified
- document what data the customer needs before ending the pilot

Safe wording:

```text
If the pilot is not a fit, we will work with you to close it cleanly and preserve or export the records that matter for the workflow we tested where practical.
```

## Security And Trust Claims

Safe to say:

- Apex HQ uses role-based access in the app.
- Apex HQ is built around company workspace separation.
- Field roles are intentionally restricted from office/admin information.
- Security and data separation are part of every package direction.

Do not say yet:

- SOC 2 certified
- fully compliant
- bank-level security
- enterprise-ready
- HIPAA/PCI compliant
- guaranteed uptime/SLA

unless those claims are formally verified and documented.

## AI And Data

Current AI positioning must stay review-first.

Safe AI wording:

```text
Where AI help exists, it is intended to organize or draft information for human review. It should not send messages, approve work, price jobs, schedule crews, or make customer commitments automatically.
```

Do not feed unnecessary sensitive data into AI workflows.

Do not imply customer data is used for broad AI training unless a formal, reviewed policy says so.

## Early Pilot Data Checklist

Before starting a pilot:

- Selected workflow is clear.
- Customer knows this is founder-led and controlled.
- Only needed users and records are added.
- Field role boundaries are understood.
- Customer knows real data will not be used publicly without permission.
- Support contact path is clear.
- Day-3 and day-10 review dates are set.

## Open Items Before Wider Launch

Prepare or review before public scale:

- formal privacy policy
- formal terms of service
- data retention/deletion policy
- incident response contact/process
- integration data handling rules
- SMS/email consent and opt-out rules
- AI data use disclosure
- customer export/offboarding process
