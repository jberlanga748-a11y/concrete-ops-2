## What This Helps With

Apex HQ is meant to help the business stop running from scattered texts, paper notes, memory, Facebook messages, Wix leads, phone photos, and manual follow-up.

The main goal is:

```text
Lead -> Estimate -> Job -> Schedule -> Field Proof -> Change Order -> Closeout -> Owner Review
```

For a growing fence company, that means:

- leads are easier to track
- estimates are easier to prepare and follow up on
- jobs are easier to hand off to the crew
- photos and proof stay attached to the job
- change orders get reviewed before they turn into arguments
- owner can see what needs attention
- employees can see their work without seeing office/pricing/admin areas
- payroll/time review is cleaner before final payroll is handled

## Why The Price Makes Sense

If this was pieced together with separate tools, a contractor could easily be over $1,000/month before setup and support.

Comparable outside stack:

```text
Jobber-style CRM / scheduling / quote workflow: $199-$699/month depending on plan
ArcSite-style takeoff / estimate tool: about $120-$160/month
CompanyCam-style photo documentation: about $63-$199/month
Gusto-style payroll provider: about $49-$180/month plus per-person fees
Website management: commonly $300-$500/month
Setup/help/support: extra
```

That kind of stack can land around:

```text
$1,100-$1,300/month
```

And that still does not include John setting it up around the actual workflow.

The founder price is:

```text
$999/month
```

That is an early customer price because the customer is coming in early, giving feedback, and helping shape the workflow.

## Important Boundaries

Say this clearly:

```text
I do not want to overpromise this. Apex HQ is built to help organize and speed up the workflow, but it does not guarantee leads, jobs, revenue, or a million-dollar result.
```

AI boundary:

```text
The AI helps draft, organize, score, flag, and prepare work. Important actions should still be reviewed before anything gets sent, priced, scheduled, billed, or changed.
```

Fence measuring boundary:

```text
The satellite fence measuring tool helps with rough takeoffs and first looks. Final measurements should still be verified onsite before contract, ordering, or install.
```

Payroll boundary:

```text
Apex HQ can help track time and prepare payroll review, but final payroll, taxes, filings, payments, workers comp, and compliance should still go through the payroll provider, accountant, or proper business process.
```

Website boundary:

```text
Website management covers basic updates, photos/projects, service edits, contact form checks, and normal monthly support. Full redesigns, paid ads, major SEO campaigns, large custom pages, and heavy content work are separate.
```
