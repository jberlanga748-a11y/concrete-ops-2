# Apex HQ Hiring And Delegation Plan

Status: growth operations plan

## Principle

Do not hire before the work is repeatable.

First, prove:

- who buys
- why they buy
- how onboarding works
- what support repeats
- what customers pay for

## First Help To Add

### Admin / Sales Assistant

Trigger:

- founder has demos available but tracker/follow-up work is slipping

Work:

- update tracker
- schedule demos
- send approved follow-ups
- organize notes
- prepare call lists

### Customer Success / Onboarding Help

Trigger:

- pilots/customers are stalling because founder is overloaded

Work:

- setup calls
- day-3 check-ins
- day-10 reviews
- training
- support intake

### Content / Marketing Help

Trigger:

- sales motion is working but content is inconsistent

Work:

- turn founder notes into posts
- format case studies
- prepare email/newsletter drafts

## What Founder Should Keep

- early sales calls
- demos
- pricing decisions
- strategic partnerships
- product judgment
- customer trust conversations

## What To Delegate First

- formatting docs
- tracker cleanup
- scheduling
- simple follow-up drafting
- call list prep
- customer onboarding checklist work

## Hiring Risk

Do not hire someone to solve unclear strategy.

Hire only after the work is painful, repeated, and documented.
