## FAQ Page

### Is Apex HQ for concrete only?

Apex HQ is being built concrete-first because that is the founder's background. It may also fit hardscape, excavation, remodel, and small GC workflows where estimates, job handoff, photos, reports, and follow-up matter.

### Is Apex HQ fully launched?

Apex HQ is in a founder-led pilot stage. That means early contractors can test focused workflows and give direct feedback before wider rollout.

### What does the founder pilot cost?

Founder pricing is discussed after fit is confirmed. The first step is a walkthrough and a practical pilot conversation.

### Does Apex HQ guarantee more leads or revenue?

No. Apex HQ helps organize contractor work and follow-up. It does not guarantee lead volume, sales, or revenue.

### Does Apex HQ replace QuickBooks, payroll, or accounting?

No. Apex HQ is not being sold as an accounting or payroll replacement.

### Does Apex HQ send emails or texts automatically?

Not in the founder pilot. Communication should be review-first so messages are checked before anything goes out.

### Can the field crew use it?

Yes, the goal is to keep field actions simple: see the job, upload proof, complete a report or update, and keep the office informed.

### Do we need to move our whole company in right away?

No. The recommended start is one real workflow with one owner/admin and, if needed, one field user or foreman.

### What happens after the pilot?

At the end of the pilot, the contractor and founder decide whether to continue, adjust the workflow, or stop. No fake pressure. The pilot should prove useful on real work.
